## Story

Kesh was the only one who came back from the last launch. They gave him a new suit and a new date. He hung the suit on a hook and did not put it on. The Warden said his family's debt would grow. Kesh nodded. A new recruit asked him if he was afraid. I was afraid before, Kesh said. Now I know. The rocket has the same welds and the same valves. Nothing has changed except me. The recruit sat down next to him. The next morning there were two suits on hooks. By the end of the week there were nine.

> **Wisdom:** Courage can mean staying on the ground.

### Gameplay

In a crew barracks lit by a single lamp, Kesh sits on a bunk facing a young recruit. The player chooses what Kesh says from three large speech cards, and the recruit's tense shoulders slowly relax.

### World

The debt-crew barracks beside a rocket yard: rows of iron bunks in a long tin hut, soot on every surface, patched flight suits on hooks. Through the window a riveted rocket stands under floodlights in the smog. Many bunks are neatly made and empty. A single oil lamp glows lamp orange on Kesh's bunk, and a sprouting onion sits in a tin on the sill.

### Characters and materials

Kesh, a pilot in his late twenties who looks older, wears a patched rust flight jacket with a scorched sleeve, a mustard scarf and cracked goggles on a cord. Young recruits in oversized suits, and a Warden who looks as scared as they are. Rivets, wool blankets, tin mugs, a debt card in a coat pocket. Soot, rust and ash, with one warm lamp.

### Expanded play

The story branches across the week before a launch. The player helps Kesh talk with recruits, with Old Brann, with a nervous Warden and with his own mother, who still carries the family debt. No choice ever harms anyone; choices build trust or lose it. Endings show crews refusing together, a launch delayed or Kesh teaching safety. The final view shows Kesh and recruits studying a rocket drawing.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Moral courage, saying no under pressure, solidarity**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the age and tone rules of [Shacados](../../lore/shacados.md): dark but never hopeless, people never villains, the player never harms anyone.
