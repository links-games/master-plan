# Stepping Stone Patterns

PK-06 · [Preschool (3–5)](../pk.md) · Cx 1 · Ages 3–5 · Added for the Raya feedback

**The idea:** Side view of round stepping stones crossing a shallow stream in a red, blue, red, blue pattern with one gap. The child drags the right colored stone into the gap, and Linky hops across with a happy boop.

| Decision             | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familiar mechanic    | AB and ABB pattern completion                                                                                                                                                               |
| Learning objective   | Repeating patterns, predicting what comes next                                                                                                                                              |
| First prototype      | One authored challenge built around the interaction above, with a clear goal, immediate feedback, restart and unlimited retries.                                                            |
| Technology           | React + SVG/DOM; deterministic state, undo and seeded levels. Start with one local screen and touch plus keyboard input.                                                                    |
| Scope boundary       | One board and one rule set; defer accounts, multiplayer, procedural content and platform SDKs.                                                                                              |
| Reuse                | Shared input, saves, accessibility, seeded state and the preschool (3–5) collection shell.                                                                                                  |
| Prototype acceptance | A player can identify the next action from the screen, complete one challenge, explain the practiced idea and restart without help. Check touch and keyboard play at narrow and wide sizes. |
| Main uncertainty     | Test whether the lesson follows from play and whether the first interaction is understandable without reading.                                                                              |

## Story

Linky wanted to cross the stream. The stones went red, blue, red, blue, red, and then there was a gap. "Which one comes next?" asked the Orbi. Linky looked back along the path. "Red, blue, red, blue, red," he sang. "Blue!" The Orbi lifted a blue stone and set it gently in the gap. Linky hopped across. On the other side he turned and looked at the path. It was like a song he could see. "Patterns tell me what comes next," he said.

> **Wisdom:** Look back, and you will know what comes next.

### Gameplay

Side view of round stepping stones crossing a shallow stream in a red, blue, red, blue pattern with one gap. The child drags the right colored stone into the gap, and Linky hops across with a happy boop.

### World

A shallow, sparkling stream runs across the pebble beach to join the river. Green moss and tiny white flowers grow between the stones. Blue-green trees with white lattice nests frame the scene, and a white Chord tower rises in the soft distance. Morning light is clear and gentle, with dew on every leaf.

### Characters and materials

Stepping stones are round, smooth and big, colored coral red, sky blue and leaf green, with soft gray outlines and one gentle shadow. Linky waits on the near bank, his red and blue hands raised. A white Orbi hovers over the gap, lifting the chosen stone with a faint shimmer. The water is clear turquoise over pale pebbles.

### Expanded play

Patterns grow from AB to ABB, AAB and ABC, first with colors, then with round and square shapes, then with sounds, where each stone plays a note. Later the child lays a whole pattern path, and Soru, Tari and Verbi hop across it in a line, humming the pattern out loud as they go.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Repeating patterns, predicting what comes next**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69) and keep every instruction voiced; reading is never required.
