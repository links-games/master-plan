## Story

The Orbi hummed a sound over the pool. "Mmm." Three leaf boats came drifting. One carried a fish, one a ball, and one a moon. Little Letterlink M listened to each one. "Fish. Fff. Not me." "Ball. Buh. Not me." "Moon. Mmm!" M hopped onto the moon boat and laughed. "I knew it was you. Your name starts like my sound." The boat sailed to the stone harbor, where all the M words were waiting, humming together like bees.

> **Wisdom:** Listen to the start, and words tell their names.

### Gameplay

Three broad leaf boats drift slowly in a calm river pool, each carrying a big picture: a ball, a fish and a moon. The Orbi says a sound, and the child taps the boat whose picture starts with it.

### World

A quiet bend of the river where the water forms a round, calm pool between smooth boulders. Willows drip light green leaves onto the surface, and small white lattice nests peek from their branches. A white Chord tower stands far upstream, misty in soft afternoon light. Dragonflies hover, and the pool reflects a pale blue sky.

### Characters and materials

Leaf boats are broad green leaves with curled edges, each carrying one large, clear picture of a familiar thing. Letterlinks, little letter creatures holding hands, wave from a flat rock. A white Orbi hovers low over the water, its coral-red left eye and sky-blue right eye glowing as it speaks. Soft green, water blue and sunny yellow.

### Expanded play

At first the boats carry very different sounds, like m, s and b. Later the sounds come closer, and four boats float at once. Each found boat sails to a little harbor of stones, where words that start alike wait together. Tapping the harbor plays them back, and the matching Letterlink rides on top.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Hearing the first sound of a word, early phonics**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69) and keep every instruction voiced; reading is never required.
