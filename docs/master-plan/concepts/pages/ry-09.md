## Story

"Shh," said the Orbi. "Quiet feet." The little one stepped on a flat stone. Hop. Then a log. Hop. Between the stones a beetle carried a crumb, and a tiny white flower was just opening. Hop, hop, over them both. At the top of the hill they sat very still. One rabbit came out. Then two. Then a whole family, nibbling clover. When they walked home, the meadow looked just as it had before. The flower kept opening. The beetle kept carrying. Nobody even knew they had been there.

> **Wisdom:** Walk softly. Leave the meadow whole.

### Gameplay

A side view of a flower meadow: large flat stepping stones glow softly ahead, a beetle and a tiny white flower sit between them, and a white Orbi floats along while a rabbit peeks out of the tall grass.

### World

A flower steppe in soft morning light, with waves of pink, yellow and white blossoms, a stream of round pebbles and a slender white Chord tower far away on the horizon. A herd of deer-like grazers walks slowly in the distance. No path is worn into the ground; only natural flat stones and fallen logs lead the way.

### Characters and materials

A white Orbi sphere with a coral-red left eye and a sky-blue right eye leads the walk, and its eyes curve into a smile each time a step lands on stone. The animals are round and gentle: a beetle, a snail, a rabbit, a ground bird with speckled eggs. Big soft shapes, thick rounded outlines, meadow greens and petal pinks, no text.

### Expanded play

New walks lead through a misty forest of mossy logs, a pebbly river beach with crabs, and a snowy glacier valley with paw prints. At the end of each walk a quiet viewpoint fills with the animals the child noticed, and the Orbi plays their calls. A night walk shows glowing mushrooms and the dimmed soft light of a tower crown.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Leave no trace, noticing animals, gentle movement**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Check every detail against the [Raya canon](../../lore/raya.md): untouched nature, white towers and clothing, Orbis, the Guard and the Gentle Defaults.
