## Story

Sora and Tavi wanted to play rough tag, with tackles and tumbles in the grass. Both were grown, both nineteen. Their Orbis asked each of them. "Yes," said Sora. "Yes," said Tavi. A green glow joined them, and for a while their Guards let the tumbles happen. Then Trip, who lived in the next tree, ran over. "Can I play?" The glow did not reach him. "Not this game," said Sora. "Nobody can say yes for you yet." So they switched to gentle tag, all three. Tavi tagged Trip softly, and everyone laughed.

> **Wisdom:** Your yes speaks only for you.

### Gameplay

Two young adult Rayans stand in a forest clearing with their Orbis; between them float round picture cards of rough tag, a prank, a heron nest and a small child, ready to drag into a leafy yes bowl or a stone never bowl.

### World

A forest clearing with flat stones under a slender white tower in late afternoon, sunlight slanting between tall blue-green trunks. A heron wades in a nearby pond, a younger child plays with pebbles at the edge, and walkers pass on a deer trail. The clearing feels like an open-air game field with nothing built in it.

### Characters and materials

Sora, in a fitted white lattice suit with soft gold accent lines, and her friend Tavi, in a white lattice suit with a green belt; both look about 19. Their Orbis glow soft green when both say yes, and the glow fades the moment either one changes their mind. Cards are round picture tiles; the two bowls are woven white lattice, one leafy, one stone.

### Expanded play

Later puzzles add a group waiver for a team game where anyone can leave at any time, a waiver that ends mid-game with a single thought, a walker who wanders into the field, and a festival where the players must find which rules protect children, people outside the game, wild animals and shared nature, the rules no one can waive.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Mutual consent, rights and their limits, protecting others**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Check every detail against the [Raya canon](../../lore/raya.md): untouched nature, white towers and clothing, Orbis, the Guard and the Gentle Defaults.
