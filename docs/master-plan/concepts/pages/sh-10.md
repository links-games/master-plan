## Story

The rocket was due to fly at dawn. The Rocket Hold sent Brann to sign the safety sheet, as he had signed a hundred before. He walked under the rocket with his lantern and found a weld he did not trust, then another, then a valve that hissed. He thought of the crews, young Hands flying to clear their families' debts. He knew what refusing could cost him. He took out his red chalk, circled every fault and wrote one word on the sheet. No. In the morning the rocket stayed on the ground, and three young people went home to breakfast.

> **Wisdom:** A safe no beats a fast yes.

### Gameplay

Under a riveted kerosene rocket on a cracked pad, the player sweeps a lantern across seams, valves and welds, circling each fault in red chalk, while a blank inspection sheet waits unsigned on a clipboard.

### World

A launch yard of the Rocket Hold at dusk: a tall rocket of riveted steel plates stands on a pad of cracked concrete, tied to a rusted gantry. Kerosene drips into oily puddles. Gas flares burn on the horizon, and the shanty town of the debt crews huddles beyond the fence. Old Brann's lantern is the one warm orange light, and a dandelion grows from a crack in the pad.

### Characters and materials

Old Brann, a former rocket welder with burned hands, wears a long patched mustard coat, a leather apron and goggles. Beside him the player's apprentice holds the red chalk. Rivets, weld beads, rubber gaskets, round pressure gauges with blank faces, kerosene cans, ropes and pulleys. Rust, soot and ash-gray steel, with bright red chalk circles and one warm lantern.

### Expanded play

Later rockets hide subtler faults: a weld that cracks only when cold, a valve fitted backwards, a fuel line rubbed thin by a shaking strut, a crew couch with no straps. The player learns to listen to pumps and tap welds by ear. After every rocket the same quiet choice returns: sign, or refuse and explain. The final view shows a young crew walking home for breakfast.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Engineering safety, systematic checking, moral courage**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the age and tone rules of [Shacados](../../lore/shacados.md): dark but never hopeless, people never villains, the player never harms anyone.
