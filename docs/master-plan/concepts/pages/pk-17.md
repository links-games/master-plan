# Plush Patch Clinic

PK-17 · [Preschool (3–5)](../pk.md) · Cx 2 · Ages 3–5 · Added for the Raya feedback

**The idea:** A plush grazer fawn with a torn ear lies on a soft cushion in a nest clinic. The Orbi scans it with a gentle blue glow, and the child drags a soft patch onto the ear to mend it.

| Decision             | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familiar mechanic    | Pretend doctor for plush toys                                                                                                                                                               |
| Learning objective   | Caring for others, body parts, feeling brave                                                                                                                                                |
| First prototype      | One authored challenge built around the interaction above, with a clear goal, immediate feedback, restart and unlimited retries.                                                            |
| Technology           | React + SVG/DOM; deterministic state, undo and seeded levels. Start with one local screen and touch plus keyboard input.                                                                    |
| Scope boundary       | One board and one rule set; defer accounts, multiplayer, procedural content and platform SDKs.                                                                                              |
| Reuse                | Shared input, saves, accessibility, seeded state and the preschool (3–5) collection shell.                                                                                                  |
| Prototype acceptance | A player can identify the next action from the screen, complete one challenge, explain the practiced idea and restart without help. Check touch and keyboard play at narrow and wide sizes. |
| Main uncertainty     | Test whether the lesson follows from play and whether the first interaction is understandable without reading.                                                                              |

## Story

Little Turtle the plush had a hole in his shell. His stuffing peeked out. "Will it hurt?" he whispered. The Orbi floated close. "First I will only look. May I?" Little Turtle nodded, and the Orbi scanned him with a soft blue glow. "Just a small tear." Itsy held his flipper. The Orbi held the edges together, and a soft patch went on. "All done," said the Orbi. Little Turtle looked at his shell. "I was scared, but you asked me first and told me what you would do."

> **Wisdom:** Ask first, and fear gets smaller.

### Gameplay

A plush grazer fawn with a torn ear lies on a soft cushion in a nest clinic. The Orbi scans it with a gentle blue glow, and the child drags a soft patch onto the ear to mend it.

### World

A cozy white lattice nest high in a riverside tree, set up as a little toy clinic. Round windows show the pebble beach, the river below and a white Chord tower in the distance. Soft afternoon light falls on shelves of cushions, blankets and toys. Warm, calm and caring. Eye-level camera inside the nest.

### Characters and materials

Plush patients are soft felt toys: a fawn, a heron, a turtle and a round grazer, with button eyes and stitched seams. Tools are big and friendly: the Orbi's scanning glow, soft patches, a warm blanket, a cup of herb tea. Itsy gives hugs. The white Orbi's coral-red and sky-blue eyes shine kindly. Cream, soft pink and leaf green.

### Expanded play

Each plush has one problem at first: a torn seam, a sniffly cold, a lost button. Later plushes have two problems, and the child chooses what to do first. A waiting cushion shows who is next, and mended toys ride home to their nests. A free mode lets the child invent a new plush and its little owie.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Caring for others, body parts, feeling brave**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69) and keep every instruction voiced; reading is never required.
