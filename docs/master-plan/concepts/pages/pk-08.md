## Story

A little river chick wandered off to see the water. When it turned around, it saw only reeds. "Peep?" it said. The Orbi floated down. "I know the way, but you can find it too. Follow the sand." The chick walked one way and met a rock. It walked another way and met the water. Then it found the soft sand path between the reeds and followed it, turn by turn, to its mother's nest. Itsy was waiting there with a hug. "You found your own way home," she said.

> **Wisdom:** A wrong turn helps you learn the road.

### Gameplay

High view of a wide sandy path winding between reeds and pebbles. The child slides one finger along it, guiding a fluffy river chick from the water's edge to its mother's nest in the grass.

### World

A sunny stretch of riverbank where tall reeds and pebble mounds make gentle curving lanes. Butterflies drift across a bright sky. White lattice nests hang in the trees above, and a white Chord tower stands beyond them. The light is warm and wide, and the river shines softly at the bottom of the picture.

### Characters and materials

The path is wide pale sand with soft edges, three finger widths across. A gray-and-cream river chick with orange feet follows the finger, peeping. Reeds are green with brown tufts. A white Orbi hovers ahead as a guide, eyes glowing coral red and sky blue, and Itsy, the hugging loop friend, waits at the nest.

### Expanded play

Mazes grow from one bend to two or three choices, then to little loops with stepping stones and log bridges. At a dead end the chick turns around with a giggle and a voiced hint, never a failure. Later the child can draw a new path for a friend and choose which baby animal goes home next.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Planning a path, finger control, trying again**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69) and keep every instruction voiced; reading is never required.
