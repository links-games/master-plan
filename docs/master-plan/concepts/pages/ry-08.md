## Story

Ana wanted her nest in the old giant tree. "Ask first," said her Orbi, and together they looked closely. A bird had eggs on the east side. A young branch was still growing on the west. So Ana chose four thick, old branches and touched each one with only a soft cradle of lattice: no nails, no ropes that squeeze. The Orbis floated the strips into place one by one. Years later the tree had grown taller, and the nest had moved with it twice. Not one ring of bark was ever scarred.

> **Wisdom:** Build lightly. The tree was here first.

### Gameplay

Inside a huge blue-green tree crown, the player drags light white lattice strips that an Orbi floats into place; strong old branches glow green where a strip may rest, while growing tips and a bird nest glow amber.

### World

A temperate rainforest of blue-green giant trees at midday, with light falling in long shafts through layers of leaves. High in one crown a half-built white nest is taking shape. Beyond the canopy a single white Chord tower rises above the mist, and far below a stream runs over mossy stones, with no paths or roads anywhere.

### Characters and materials

Ana, in a white lattice suit with a seasonal over-layer and a small round pin, guides two white Orbis that lift lattice strips and rings of grown wood, a few kilograms each at most. The bark is deep brown with moss; the lattice is pearly white with a fine hexagon texture. A squirrel-like climber and a nesting bird watch from nearby branches.

### Expanded play

Later seasons make the tree grow: branches thicken and rise, and the nest must be gently loosened and moved so it never squeezes the bark. New sites appear on a cliff ledge and under a tower's rings, storms test the balance, and a final mode asks the player to take a nest apart and leave no mark at all.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Balance and structure, building without harming nature**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Check every detail against the [Raya canon](../../lore/raya.md): untouched nature, white towers and clothing, Orbis, the Guard and the Gentle Defaults.
