## Story

One morning, the little Orbi of the meadow blew a bubble. It was round and shiny, and it floated up, up, up. Linky reached out one finger. Pop! A shower of tiny sparkles fell on the clover. A fawn sneezed, and Linky laughed. The Orbi blew another bubble, and another, slow and soft. Linky did not grab. Linky touched each one gently, and each one sang a tiny note before it popped. Soon the whole meadow was humming. Then the sun grew warm, and everyone lay down for a rest.

> **Wisdom:** Gentle hands, happy friends.

### Gameplay

Big soft bubbles in coral, leaf green, sky blue and sunshine yellow drift up over a clover meadow; one small finger touch pops a bubble into sparkles while a fawn watches and Linky giggles.

### World

A wide, low clover meadow in the morning, dew shining on round leaves. At the far edge a short white Chord tower rises like a smooth plant stem with ringed nodes and a soft glow at its crown. Fawns graze in the mist, wildflowers sway, and the pale sky leaves plenty of empty space for bubbles to float.

### Characters and materials

Linky sits in the clover, a round chibi friend with a coral-red left eye, a sky-blue right eye and an arrow-smile. A white Orbi sphere with a glowing equator ring blows bubbles through a gentle ring of light. Bubbles are glossy and see-through with rainbow rims. Flat fills, thick rounded gray outlines, soft pastel palette.

### Expanded play

Later scenes open with new kinds of bubbles: dusk bubbles that glow like lanterns, giant slow bubbles that split into three small ones, and round balloons that float up and open into quiet light blossoms over the tower. Each bubble sings one soft note, so a bubble storm becomes a little melody. A sleepy goodbye scene ends every play.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Cause and effect, pointing, colors**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69): tap before drag, no reading, no failure, co-play under age 2.
