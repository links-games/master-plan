## Story

The meadow was very quiet. Linky found a row of shiny stones in the grass. Linky tapped the first one. Ding! A tiny flower popped up. Linky tapped the last one. Dong! That one was low and deep, like a frog. Ding, dong, ding. A bird answered from the tree. Verbi hopped over and drummed on a log. Bum, bum. Soon the bird, the frog, Verbi and Linky made a song together. Nobody played it wrong, because every note was a friend.

> **Wisdom:** Every sound is a little song.

### Gameplay

Eight wide rainbow chime stones fill the screen like stepping stones across the clover; a tap on any stone rings a soft note and a little flower blooms above it.

### World

A flower steppe meadow in golden afternoon light, with the short white Chord tower in the distance glowing faintly as if it is listening. Butterflies drift, grass ripples in waves of wind, and the sky is warm apricot. Wide open space and gentle light make the scene feel like a quiet outdoor music room.

### Characters and materials

The chime stones are smooth, wide pebbles in coral, orange, yellow, leaf green, teal, sky blue, violet and pink, lying in a row. Linky bounces from stone to stone, and Verbi, the green triangle, taps a round drum made from a hollow log. An Orbi floats like a conductor with blinking red and blue eyes. Rounded, toy-like shapes.

### Expanded play

New instruments join as the child plays: a set of log drums, a leaf rattle, a frog that croaks the low notes and birds that sing the high ones. All notes come from one gentle scale, so every tap sounds pretty. A play-along mode lets a parent tap a song while fawns dance; nothing is ever wrong.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Sound, rhythm, high and low notes**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69): tap before drag, no reading, no failure, co-play under age 2.
