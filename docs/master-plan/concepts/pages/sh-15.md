## Story

The rain on Shacados stings the eyes and burns the leaves. Grandfather Tobb built a greenhouse on the roof anyway, out of every broken window he could find. The panes did not match. Some were green, some were cracked, one was the windshield of a truck. When the rain was too sour, his granddaughter crushed eggshells and stirred them in. When the test turned purple, she watered the beans. In autumn they had a basket of beans and three fat tubers. Tobb gave the first basket to the neighbors who had brought the glass.

> **Wisdom:** Broken glass can still hold a garden.

### Gameplay

A rooftop greenhouse of mismatched scrap glass. The player fits panes into a rusted frame, catches sour rain in barrels, stirs in crushed eggshell and ash until a cabbage-juice test turns from pink to purple, and watches tuber sprouts open.

### World

A flat tenement rooftop in the warm belt, surrounded by chimneys and laundry lines under a brown sky. Acid rain streaks the soot on the walls. In the middle stands a greenhouse built from window glass, bottle bottoms and an old truck windshield in an iron frame, its seams patched with tar. A lantern inside glows lamp orange, and green leaves press against the fogged panes.

### Characters and materials

A grandfather and his granddaughter in patched rust and ash coats, boots cut from old tires, scarves and goggles. Neighbors hand up buckets of ash and eggshells. Mismatched glass, tar seams, a rusted iron frame, rain barrels, jars of red cabbage juice for testing, crates of soil, tuber sprouts and beans on strings. Sick green leaves turning healthy green, with one lamp.

### Expanded play

As seasons pass the greenhouse grows: a worm bin for richer soil, a filter for the rain barrels, a bed of cave fungus underneath, and new crops like beans, kale and a tiny apple seedling. Neighbors build their own glasshouses on nearby roofs. The final view shows a skyline of glinting scrap-glass gardens between the chimneys.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Soil acidity, plant needs, building with salvage**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the age and tone rules of [Shacados](../../lore/shacados.md): dark but never hopeless, people never villains, the player never harms anyone.
