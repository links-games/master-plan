## Story

Linky looked into the pond. A face looked back. Linky smiled, and the face smiled. Then Linky made a sad face, because a bubble had popped. The face in the pond was sad too. Then the fawn came and nuzzled Linky. Linky's face changed into a little smile. Feelings come and go, like ripples on the water. All feelings are okay, and a friend can help a sad feeling feel better.

> **Wisdom:** All feelings are okay.

### Gameplay

A round pond mirror shows Linky's face looking happy; below it float two big face bubbles, happy and sleepy, and the child taps happy, so Linky laughs and the pond sparkles.

### World

A still, clear pond in the clover meadow that works like a gentle mirror, ringed with smooth white stones. The short white Chord tower is reflected in the water. Soft morning light, a breeze that ripples the surface now and then, and birdsong. Calm and safe.

### Characters and materials

Linky shows big, readable feelings with its red and blue eyes and arrow-smile: happy, sad, surprised, sleepy, cross, and scared then comforted. The face bubbles are big round icons. An Orbi offers an optional mirror that shows the child's own face in the pond, on the device only and never saved.

### Expanded play

Later, more friends show feelings, a fawn, a duck and Oopsy, and the child chooses among three faces. Little scenes ask how a friend feels when a balloon floats away or when a friend gets a hug. A parent-only mirror mode lets the child make faces together with Linky. Sad feelings always end comforted.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Naming feelings, facial expressions, empathy**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69): tap before drag, no reading, no failure, co-play under age 2.
