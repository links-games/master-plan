## Story

For a year the listeners on the water tower decoded the pattern from the faint star. Two, three, five, seven. Then pictures made of dots: a tree, a person, an open, empty hand. Now they had to answer. Brann wanted to warn them about the probe the Holders had launched. Wren wanted to teach them our numbers. Sela wanted to send a seed. They argued until dawn. In the end they sent all three in one message, with every neighbor's hand on the key. The reply would take years to arrive. Good, said Sela. Then we have years to become worth meeting.

> **Wisdom:** Answer the dark with an open hand.

### Gameplay

In a radio shack on a water tower, the player punches dots into a paper tape to build a simple picture grid, then gathers neighbors' hands on one shared telegraph key to send the reply toward a faint star.

### World

A hidden radio station on a water tower above the scrap dunes, built from salvaged dish reflectors and copper wire, on a clear cold night after a storm. Below, the town sleeps under smoke and gas-flare glow. Above, one faint star is Raya's sun. A kerosene lamp glows lamp orange inside the shack, and a small fern grows in a can by its door.

### Characters and materials

Teyo, now older, Wren with her slate, Old Brann at the transmitter, Sela holding a jar of seeds and a dozen neighbors crowded into the shack. Patched coats, scarves and goggles pushed up. Vacuum tubes, punched paper tape, a hand-wound coil, a brass telegraph key and a chalk grid of dots on a board. Soot, rust, mustard and ash, with one warm lamp.

### Expanded play

The campaign grows from decoding the Chord's pulses to building a stronger transmitter from town salvage, composing a reply in primes and dot pictures, and keeping the station hidden from the Radio Hold. The town debates what to send: a warning about the probe, a greeting or a picture of a seed. The final view shows the reply leaving as faint rings over the dunes, years from arrival.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Universal messages, primes, picture grids, light delay, consensus**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the age and tone rules of [Shacados](../../lore/shacados.md): dark but never hopeless, people never villains, the player never harms anyone.
