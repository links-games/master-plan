## Story

The meadow woke up without colors. The fawn was white. The sky was white. Even the clover was white! Linky picked up a drop of blue and touched the sky. Splash! The sky was blue. Linky touched the clover with green. Splash! Linky made the fawn orange with white spots. And then Linky made one cloud pink, just because. The fawn liked its new color and jumped for joy. A world is happier with everyone's colors.

> **Wisdom:** Add your color to the world.

### Gameplay

A big line drawing of a fawn under a tree has only four large parts; a tap on the sky pours sky-blue paint in with a splash, while five fat paint drops wait at the bottom to pick another color.

### World

The coloring pictures show the toddler meadow itself: clover, the short white Chord tower, fawns, bubbles and ponds, all drawn as thick, friendly line art. Around the canvas, a pale lattice frame and a calm cream background keep the focus on the picture. Gentle, even morning light.

### Characters and materials

Five huge paint drops sit at the bottom: coral, sunshine yellow, leaf green, sky blue and violet. Pictures have three to six big parts with very thick rounded outlines, so every tap lands. Linky cheers with red and blue hands when a part fills. Soft paint splash particles and a glossy finish.

### Expanded play

Picture packs grow: animals, friends, the night sky and the toy parade. Parts get a little smaller as the child grows, and a magic brush fills with gentle rainbow stripes. Finished pictures come alive with a small animation, the fawn wags its tail, the bubble floats away, and they collect in a meadow album.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Colors, choosing, finishing a picture**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69): tap before drag, no reading, no failure, co-play under age 2.
