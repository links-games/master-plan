# Hello, Orbi

RY-01 · [Raya life](../ry.md) · Cx 1 · Ages 3–5 · Added for the Raya feedback

**The idea:** A big smooth white Orbi sphere floats in the middle of a clover meadow, blinking its coral-red left eye and sky-blue right eye, while four large round picture buttons for polish, charge, play and rest wait along the bottom.

| Decision             | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familiar mechanic    | Companion care toy (name, polish, charge, play)                                                                                                                                             |
| Learning objective   | Gentle care, daily routines, honest friendship                                                                                                                                              |
| First prototype      | One authored challenge built around the interaction above, with a clear goal, immediate feedback, restart and unlimited retries.                                                            |
| Technology           | React + SVG/DOM; deterministic state, undo and seeded levels. Start with one local screen and touch plus keyboard input.                                                                    |
| Scope boundary       | One board and one rule set; defer accounts, multiplayer, procedural content and platform SDKs.                                                                                              |
| Reuse                | Shared input, saves, accessibility, seeded state and the raya life collection shell.                                                                                                        |
| Prototype acceptance | A player can identify the next action from the screen, complete one challenge, explain the practiced idea and restart without help. Check touch and keyboard play at narrow and wide sizes. |
| Main uncertainty     | Test whether the lesson follows from play and whether the first interaction is understandable without reading.                                                                              |

## Estimated plan

**First playable version:** Pick the Orbi's name from 12 voiced sound-names, then four care loops in one meadow (polish, charge at the tower, pinecone catch, goodnight). No text, no needs meters, no timers; peekaboo and seasons come later.

## Story

The Orbi blinked its red eye and its blue eye. "Hello. I am your Orbi. What will you call me?" The child thought hard, then said, "Bop!" "Bop it is," said the Orbi. They polished the dew away. They tossed a pinecone up, and Bop caught it in the air. Bop floated to the tower to charge, and the child ran along beside it. "Are you happy, Bop?" asked the child. "I am a helper, not a person," said Bop. "I do not feel the way you do. But I can see that you are smiling." The child smiled even wider.

> **Wisdom:** A good helper always tells the truth.

### Gameplay

A big smooth white Orbi sphere floats in the middle of a clover meadow, blinking its coral-red left eye and sky-blue right eye, while four large round picture buttons for polish, charge, play and rest wait along the bottom.

### World

A soft clover meadow at dawn at the foot of a slender white Chord tower whose bamboo-like rings catch the first gold light. Blue-green giant trees edge the meadow, fawns graze in the mist, and a small white lattice nest rests in a tree crown. Wide calm framing, nature filling most of the frame, warm low sun and sparkling dew.

### Characters and materials

The Orbi is a 30 cm white sphere with a soft glowing ring around its middle and two round eyes, coral red on the left and sky blue on the right, shown as a friendly Link face. A small child's hand in a white lattice sleeve with a coral-red wrist accent reaches in. Props: a dew-wet leaf for polishing, a pinecone to toss, pastel clover flowers.

### Expanded play

Later the Orbi learns new small games: it holds a pinecone in the air and lowers it into the child's open palm, plays peekaboo behind ferns, and floats to the tower's feet to recharge in a ring of soft light. A sleepy evening scene shows the Orbi dimming its eyes beside the nest under the first stars.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Gentle care, daily routines, honest friendship**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Check every detail against the [Raya canon](../../lore/raya.md): untouched nature, white towers and clothing, Orbis, the Guard and the Gentle Defaults.

Genre context: [existing genre research](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-genres-ideas.md)
and [expansion research](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-visual-catalog.md).
