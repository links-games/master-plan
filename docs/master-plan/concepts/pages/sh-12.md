## Story

The smoke came on a dry afternoon. A hot cinder had rolled from a slag heap into the grass. The bell rang in the watch hut, and everyone in the valley came running, even the smallest children. They did not rush at the fire. They cleared a line of bare earth and passed buckets from the stream to keep it wet. The fire reached the line and stopped. The next week the children came back with seedlings in tin cans. A scorched patch, Sela told them, is only a garden that has not started yet.

> **Wisdom:** Every ash patch can grow green again.

### Gameplay

A grid map of the Last Woods seen from above, with a thin line of smoke creeping in from a slag heap. The player taps big tiles to clear firebreaks, sends a bucket line from the stream, then plants saplings in the scorched squares.

### World

The Last Woods at the edge of a coal district: a valley of birches and pines, thin but alive, ringed by smoking slag heaps. A hot cinder has rolled into dry grass at the valley mouth, and gray smoke drifts low. Downhill, a stream still runs clear over stones. A watch hut's lantern glows lamp orange, and a young fern grows by its door.

### Characters and materials

Sela and the valley watchers in patched wool and canvas, damp scarves over their faces and goggles on. Children pass buckets hand to hand from the stream while an elder rakes leaves off a path. Tin buckets, shovels, wet sacks, a bell on a post and seedlings in tin cans. Rust, ash, soot and sick green, with one warm lantern and no flames near people.

### Expanded play

After each dry season the player replants: hardy grasses first, then birches, then slow oaks from Sela's top shelf. Scorched tiles turn green over the years. New valleys join the watch, and the player builds stone firebreaks and small ponds. The final view shows the valley from a ridge, green creeping up an old slag heap and a hawk circling above.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **How fires spread, firebreaks, forest regrowth**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the age and tone rules of [Shacados](../../lore/shacados.md): dark but never hopeless, people never villains, the player never harms anyone.
