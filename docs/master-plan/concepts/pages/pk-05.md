# Rela's Two Sides

PK-05 · [Preschool (3–5)](../pk.md) · Cx 1 · Ages 3–5 · Added for the Raya feedback

**The idea:** Rela, a round two-faced moon creature, floats over the beach showing a full basket of berries. Three big picture pebbles wait below: an empty basket, a tree and a fish. The child taps the empty basket, and Rela turns around to show it.

| Decision             | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familiar mechanic    | Opposites matching                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Learning objective   | Opposites: big and small, full and empty, up and down                                                                                                                                       |
| First prototype      | One authored challenge built around the interaction above, with a clear goal, immediate feedback, restart and unlimited retries.                                                            |
| Technology           | React + SVG/DOM; deterministic state, undo and seeded levels. Start with one local screen and touch plus keyboard input.                                                                    |
| Scope boundary       | One board and one rule set; defer accounts, multiplayer, procedural content and platform SDKs.                                                                                              |

## Story

Rela the moon has two faces. On one side she is bright and wide awake. On the other side she is dark and sleepy. A little crab asked, "Which one is the real you?" Rela smiled with both faces. "Both. I am full and I am empty. I am up and I am down. Every side needs its other side." The crab thought about it. "Then I am awake now, and sleepy later, and still me." "Yes," said Rela, and turned around to show him the stars.

> **Wisdom:** Every side has another side.

### Gameplay

Rela, a round two-faced moon creature, floats over the beach showing a full basket of berries. Three big picture pebbles wait below: an empty basket, a tree and a fish. The child taps the empty basket, and Rela turns around to show it.

### World

The pebble beach at dusk, when the sky turns peach and lilac. The two small moons, Pim and Pom, rise above the river. A white Chord tower glows softly at its crown, dimmed for the animals. Trees on the bank hold white lattice nests lit by tiny warm lights. The water is calm and mirror-smooth. Wide, quiet composition.

### Characters and materials

Rela is a friendly chibi moon with a pale cream front face and a soft blue back face, each with big gentle eyes. Picture pebbles are chunky, round and smooth, each showing one clear image. A white Orbi floats beside Rela, its coral-red and sky-blue eyes reflected in the water. Soft gray outlines; peach, lilac, cream and blue.

### Expanded play

Pairs grow from big and small to in and out, wet and dry, fast and slow, near and far. Later the child acts out opposites by dragging: a frog jumps into the pond, then out again. A night round uses the moons Pim and Pom, one bright and one in shadow, to show light and dark.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Opposites: big and small, full and empty, up and down**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69) and keep every instruction voiced; reading is never required.
