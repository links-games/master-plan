# Pebble Dot Friends

PK-12 · [Preschool (3–5)](../pk.md) · Cx 1 · Ages 3–5 · Added for the Raya feedback

**The idea:** Ten pale pebbles lie on the sand in a loose shape, and the next one glows yellow. The child taps them in order as the Orbi counts aloud, and a thread of light joins them until a heron appears and flies up.

| Decision             | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familiar mechanic    | Dot-to-dot counting from 1 to 10                                                                                                                                                            |
| Learning objective   | Counting in order from 1 to 10, number names                                                                                                                                                |
| First prototype      | One authored challenge built around the interaction above, with a clear goal, immediate feedback, restart and unlimited retries.                                                            |
| Technology           | React + SVG/DOM; deterministic state, undo and seeded levels. Start with one local screen and touch plus keyboard input.                                                                    |
| Scope boundary       | One board and one rule set; defer accounts, multiplayer, procedural content and platform SDKs.                                                                                              |
| Reuse                | Shared input, saves, accessibility, seeded state and the preschool (3–5) collection shell.                                                                                                  |
| Prototype acceptance | A player can identify the next action from the screen, complete one challenge, explain the practiced idea and restart without help. Check touch and keyboard play at narrow and wide sizes. |
| Main uncertainty     | Test whether the lesson follows from play and whether the first interaction is understandable without reading.                                                                              |

## Story

One evening, ten pebbles began to glow on the beach. "Let's join them!" said Verbi. "One!" He hopped to the first. "Two! Three!" Each pebble lit a thread of light to the next. "Four, five, six!" The line bent and curled. "Seven, eight, nine, ten!" The last pebble shone, and the line became a heron. It shook its wings and flew over the river. Verbi waved. "Counting in order made a friend," he said, and the ten pebbles dimmed, ready for tomorrow.

> **Wisdom:** One step at a time, and the picture appears.

### Gameplay

Ten pale pebbles lie on the sand in a loose shape, and the next one glows yellow. The child taps them in order as the Orbi counts aloud, and a thread of light joins them until a heron appears and flies up.

### World

The pebble beach in the blue hour after sunset, the sand cool and pale. The river is still and reflects a deep blue sky with the first stars. A white Chord tower glows softly at its crown, and white lattice nests show tiny warm lights in the trees. Peaceful, quiet and a little magical. Top-down camera over the sand.

### Characters and materials

Dot pebbles are round and pale, each glowing sunshine yellow when it is next. A thread of light, red to blue like a Link, joins tapped pebbles. Finished shapes become chibi animals: a heron, a turtle, a fish. Verbi, the springy green triangle, hops along the line. A white Orbi floats above, eyes coral red and sky blue.

### Expanded play

Pictures start with three dots and grow to ten. Each finished animal comes alive and joins a gentle night parade along the river. Later the child counts back from five to send up a soft burst of tower light, and a free mode lets the child place their own pebbles for Verbi to join.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Counting in order from 1 to 10, number names**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69) and keep every instruction voiced; reading is never required.
