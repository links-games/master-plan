**The idea:** Friends sit on round blankets in a circle on the beach, each with a few things to share. Verbi shows a thought bubble of a kite. The child drags a spare kite to him, and he gives back a wooden whistle.

| Decision             | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                    |

| First prototype      | One authored challenge built around the interaction above, with a clear goal, immediate feedback, restart and unlimited retries.                                                            |
| Technology           | React + SVG/DOM; deterministic state, undo and seeded levels. Start with one local screen and touch plus keyboard input.                                                                    |
| Scope boundary       | One board and one rule set; defer accounts, multiplayer, procedural content and platform SDKs.                                                                                              |

## Estimated plan

**First playable version:** Sharing circle with five friends showing thought bubbles; the child drags a matching gift and gets one back, with share, swap and turn-taking rounds. No coins, prices or stalls; borrowing comes later.

## Story

It was sharing day on the beach. Anya the star had a basket of seeds but wished for a kite. Verbi had two kites but wished for seeds for his garden. "Would you like to swap?" asked Anya. "Yes!" said Verbi, and gave her a kite. She gave him a handful of seeds. Then Linky came with empty hands. "I have nothing to share," he said sadly. "You can share a song," said Anya. Linky sang, and everyone clapped. On Raya nothing is bought. Everyone has something to give.

> **Wisdom:** Everyone has something to share.

### Gameplay

Friends sit on round blankets in a circle on the beach, each with a few things to share. Verbi shows a thought bubble of a kite. The child drags a spare kite to him, and he gives back a wooden whistle.

### World

A wide pebble beach where friends lay round woven blankets in a big circle near the water. Baskets and toys rest on the blankets. A white Chord tower stands nearby, and the trees hold white lattice nests with soft ribbons. Warm late afternoon sun and a gentle breeze. A friendly sharing day under an open sky. High, wide camera.

### Characters and materials

Linky, Soru, Tari, Verbi and Anya, the shape-shifting star, sit on blankets with toys, copied fruit, seeds and drawings to share. Big thought bubbles show what each friend would like. A white Orbi floats in the middle, eyes coral red and sky blue. There are no coins, prices or counters, only open hands and baskets.

### Expanded play

At first each friend wants one thing and gives one thing back. Later friends borrow and return, take turns with one toy, or split one thing between two. Some rounds the child simply gives and receives a thank-you song. A copy corner lets the Orbi make a second toy overnight, so two friends can both play.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Sharing, swapping, taking turns, saying thank you**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69) and keep every instruction voiced; reading is never required.
