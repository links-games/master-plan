## Story

The Rocket Hold announced the launch day. It did not say which way the wind would blow. Ana did. She had watched the dust for twenty years. The night before, she drew a map on brown paper and knocked on every door. Uphill, she said, toward the old quarry. Wet cloth over your mouth. Bring water, not furniture. Some people laughed at her. By dawn, most of them were on the ridge, and the rest came when they saw the others go. The launch was only a far light behind the clouds. Ana counted heads twice. Everyone was there.

> **Wisdom:** Watch the wind, then walk together.

### Gameplay

A hand-drawn map of a shanty valley lies on a crate. The player reads the chalk wind arrows, draws safe routes uphill and assigns carts and helpers, so every family reaches the upwind ridge while a distant booster waits on its pad.

### World

Launch day in a shanty valley far below a ground-launched nuclear-pulse booster: a squat steel tower with a huge round pusher plate, standing on cracked concrete across the plain. Wind socks snap on poles and dust blows toward the huts. Families climb a rocky ridge upwind with bundles and goats. A lantern hanging from a cart gives one warm orange glow, and a child carries a potted seedling.

### Characters and materials

Neighbors in layered patched coats with wet scarves pulled over their mouths and goggles on. A volunteer runner with a whistle, an old woman riding in a wheelbarrow, a mender carrying a crackling radio. Hand-drawn maps on brown paper, chalk wind arrows, rope, carts, tarps and water cans. Rust, soot, mustard and ash, with one warm lantern.

### Expanded play

Later valleys bring shifting winds, a bridge too weak for carts, a clinic that must move its beds, and a family that will not leave without their goats. The player learns to read clouds and dust, place water stations and send runners. Every level ends before the launch. The final view shows the whole valley safe on the upwind ridge, sharing tea under a tarp.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Wind and fallout maps, planning, care for the vulnerable**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the age and tone rules of [Shacados](../../lore/shacados.md): dark but never hopeless, people never villains, the player never harms anyone.
