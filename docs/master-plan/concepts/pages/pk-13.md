**The idea:** Two rain puddles on flat stones shine yellow and blue. The child taps the Orbi, it carries one drop from each into an empty middle puddle, and the water swirls into bright leaf green.

## Story

After the rain, Soru found a yellow puddle and Tari found a blue one. "Mine is the best color!" said Soru. "No, mine is!" said Tari. The Orbi carried one drop of yellow and one drop of blue into a puddle between them. The water swirled and turned green, like new leaves. The twins stared. "That is not my color," said Soru, "and not yours." "It is ours," said Tari. They mixed and mixed until the beach was full of colors that no one could make alone.

> **Wisdom:** Together we make colors no one makes alone.

### Gameplay

Two rain puddles on flat stones shine yellow and blue. The child taps the Orbi, it carries one drop from each into an empty middle puddle, and the water swirls into bright leaf green.

### World

The pebble beach just after a warm summer rain, every stone wet and shining. Rain puddles sit in hollows of flat white rocks. A rainbow arcs over the river, and a white Chord tower stands beneath it, glistening. The trees drip, and white lattice nests sparkle with drops. The air looks fresh and clean.

### Characters and materials

Puddles are round and clear, tinted by the Orbi with a harmless glow color that fades away by evening: coral red, sunshine yellow and sky blue. The white Orbi carries drops with a gentle shimmer. Soru and Tari stand at the edge with little buckets. Mixed colors swirl like marbled paint. Bold gray outlines, big simple shapes.

### Expanded play

First two colors make one new color: red and yellow make orange, blue and yellow make green. Later a friend shows a wish, like a green frog who wants a green pond, and the child mixes it. A free mode lets the child splash new colors over a whole beach picture, then watch them fade at sunset.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Primary and mixed colors, cause and effect**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69) and keep every instruction voiced; reading is never required.
