## Story

Wren lost her school when the Grain Hold closed it. So she opened another in a cellar. The price was a cup of soup. Children came after their shifts, tired and dusty. One night a boy named Ollan said he would never understand numbers. Wren gave him twelve beans and asked him to share them between three friends. He did it without thinking. That is division, Wren said. You already knew it. Your hands just learned first. Ollan stared at the beans as if they were magic. Numbers do not lie, Wren said. Learn them and nobody can cheat you.

> **Wisdom:** Numbers do not lie, so learn them.

### Gameplay

In a cellar classroom, Wren points at a slate while the player picks which of three big picture cards will help a struggling child understand sharing out, and the child's face brightens as beans are split into piles.

### World

A coal cellar under a laundry in the warm belt, its walls whitewashed long ago and now gray with soot. Steam pipes run along the ceiling and drip. A single oil lamp hangs from a hook, its lamp-orange circle of light holding eight children on crates. Outside the small high window, boots pass in the smog. A pot of chives grows under the lamp.

### Characters and materials

Wren, a night-school teacher with ink-stained fingers, wears a patched mustard cardigan over two shirts and a gray scarf. Children of different ages share slates, chalk stubs and one torn primer. A soup pot rests on a brick, the price of the lessons. Beans for counting, string for measuring, chalk and paper scraps. Soot, ash and rust, with one lamp.

### Expanded play

Each night brings new learners: a miner who wants to read his own ration card, a girl who already knows multiplication and wants more, a boy too tired to listen until he has eaten. The player learns to pair older pupils with younger ones, so the cellar starts teaching itself. The final view shows a former pupil teaching her own small class under the same lamp.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Reading, arithmetic, explaining ideas to others**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the age and tone rules of [Shacados](../../lore/shacados.md): dark but never hopeless, people never villains, the player never harms anyone.
