# Climate Council

SS-11 · [Strategy and simulation](../ss.md) · Cx 5 · Original catalog

**The idea:** Compare emissions-reduction choices on a town model with an uncertainty-band chart.

| Decision             | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familiar mechanic    | Policy sim                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Learning objective   | Climate models, trade-offs                                                                                                                                                                  |
| First prototype      | One authored challenge built around the interaction above, with a clear goal, immediate feedback, restart and unlimited retries.                                                            |
| Technology           | React shell + three.js/R3F and a validated simulation adapter. Begin with a small 2D reference model before a 3D vertical slice; use measured device tiers.                                 |
| Scope boundary       | One bounded scenario and one core system; defer a full campaign, network play and production-scale content.                                                                                 |
| Reuse                | Shared input, saves, accessibility, seeded state and the strategy and simulation collection shell.                                                                                          |
| Prototype acceptance | A player can identify the next action from the screen, complete one challenge, explain the practiced idea and restart without help. Check touch and keyboard play at narrow and wide sizes. |
| Main uncertainty     | Simulation accuracy and device cost need measured evidence before extending the scenario.                                                                                                   |

## Estimated plan

**First playable version:** A 3D town model where council choices on energy, transport and food change emissions and an uncertainty-band chart over 12 decades-long scenarios. Global models and multiplayer councils are deferred.

| Measure              | Estimate                                                                                                   |

| Youngest age         | 10+                                                                                                        |
| Toddler learnability | 1 of 5 — Needs reading, precise motor control, timing pressure or abstract rules; not a toddler game (6+). |

**Algorithms:** `weather-sim` (Weather and climate, 500 LOC) · `economy-sim` (Resource economy, 600 LOC) · `camera-3d` (3D camera, 300 LOC) · `dialogue-branching` (Branching dialogue, 300 LOC)

**Assets:** 25 sprites, 8 animations, 4 backgrounds, 16 UI screens, 15 sound effects, 3 music tracks, 60 voice lines, 12 authored levels, 150 content items, 20 3D models

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Climate models, trade-offs**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
