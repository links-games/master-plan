## Story

Linky woke up and said hello to the meadow. Quack, said the duck. Ribbit, said the frog. Buzz, said the bee. Hoo, hoo, said the owl, who was very sleepy. Linky listened with both ears. Every friend has a voice, and every voice is a way to say hello. Linky tried to say hello like the duck. Quack! The duck tipped its head. Then the duck laughed in its own duck way, and they quacked together all morning long.

> **Wisdom:** Every friend has a voice.

### Gameplay

One big wild duck stands in the clover, flapping its wings mid-quack inside a soft sound ring. A tap anywhere brings the next animal waddling in to call out its own sound.

### World

A meadow at the edge of a reed pond and a birch wood, in morning light and gentle mist. A short white Chord tower stands beyond the trees with its soft crown light. There are no fences, barns or paths: wild animals wander freely and unafraid. Lily pads, reeds and a flat sunny stone give each animal its own little stage.

### Characters and materials

Wild animals drawn round and chibi: a duck, a frog, an owl, a bee, a fawn, a woolly wild cow, a wild goat, a goose and a cricket. A white Orbi floats beside them and shows a soft sound ring when an animal calls. Linky claps with its red and blue hands. Warm greens, cream and sunflower yellow, flat fills with one shadow tone.

### Expanded play

Later, new habitats join the meadow: a night wood with an owl and crickets, a rocky hill with wild goats, a pond full of frogs. A listening mode plays the sound first and the animal pops out after a pause, so the child can guess aloud with a parent. The day ends with all the animals humming one soft goodnight together.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Animal names and sounds, listening**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69): tap before drag, no reading, no failure, co-play under age 2.

and [expansion research](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-visual-catalog.md).
