# Hello, Little Fish

TD-06 · [Toddlers (1–3)](../td.md) · Cx 1 · Ages 1–3 · Added for the Raya feedback

**The idea:** A calm round pond with lily pads fills the screen; a tap anywhere on the water makes a bright fish leap from that spot in a glittering arc, wave a fin and splash back home.

| Decision             | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familiar mechanic    | Tap fishing (fish jump and say hello)                                                                                                                                                       |
| Learning objective   | Cause and effect, colors, caring for animals                                                                                                                                                |
| First prototype      | One authored challenge built around the interaction above, with a clear goal, immediate feedback, restart and unlimited retries.                                                            |
| Technology           | React + SVG/DOM; deterministic state, undo and seeded levels. Start with one local screen and touch plus keyboard input.                                                                    |
| Scope boundary       | One board and one rule set; defer accounts, multiplayer, procedural content and platform SDKs.                                                                                              |
| Reuse                | Shared input, saves, accessibility, seeded state and the toddlers (1–3) collection shell.                                                                                                   |
| Prototype acceptance | A player can identify the next action from the screen, complete one challenge, explain the practiced idea and restart without help. Check touch and keyboard play at narrow and wide sizes. |
| Main uncertainty     | Test whether the lesson follows from play and whether the first interaction is understandable without reading.                                                                              |

## Story

Linky sat by the pond. Plip! Linky touched the water. Up jumped a little gold fish, round and shiny. It waved one fin. Hello! Splash, it went back home. Plip! A blue fish jumped. Plip! A pink fish. Linky did not try to take them. Fish love the water, and the water loves the fish. Linky just said hello, and the fish said hello back. When the sky turned pink, the fish swam to their beds under the lily pads.

> **Wisdom:** Say hello, then let them go.

### Gameplay

A calm round pond with lily pads fills the screen; a tap anywhere on the water makes a bright fish leap from that spot in a glittering arc, wave a fin and splash back home.

### World

A clear pond in the clover meadow, edged with reeds, round stones and forget-me-nots. A heron stands far off, and the short white Chord tower is reflected upside down in the still water. Morning light makes the ripples sparkle, and the water is clean enough to see pebbles on the bottom.

### Characters and materials

Round chibi fish in coral, gold, teal and lilac with big friendly eyes. Linky sits on a flat stone with its feet dangling. A white Orbi hovers over the water, its red and blue eyes watching, and makes a soft shimmer where the fish jump. Glossy water, matte leaves, flat fills and gentle shadows. Nobody catches or keeps a fish.

### Expanded play

Later ponds bring new friends: a frog that jumps after the fish, a turtle that pops its head up, and a night pond where glowing fish draw rings of light. An Orbi bubble window lets the child peek under the water at a fish family, then the bubble pops and everyone swims on. The fish always stay in their pond.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Cause and effect, colors, caring for animals**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69): tap before drag, no reading, no failure, co-play under age 2.
