## Story

A little duckling was playing in the clover and wandered too far. Peep, peep! Where is my mama? The fawn said, I am not your mama, my mama is the deer. The owlet said, I am not your mama, my mama is the owl. Linky picked up the duckling, very gently. Quack! called a voice from the pond. Mama Duck! The duckling ran under her wing. Everyone has someone who loves them.

> **Wisdom:** No one is lost for long.

### Gameplay

A fluffy duckling stands in the clover, peeping; two grown animals wait nearby, a duck and a deer, and the child drags the duckling to the duck, who wraps it in a wing.

### World

A peaceful meadow with a pond, a birch grove and a gentle hill below the short white Chord tower. Late afternoon warmth, with wild animals resting together in families. Nature fills the whole frame: reeds, clover, wildflowers and soft clouds.

### Characters and materials

Families: duck and duckling, deer and fawn, owl and owlet, otter and pup, goose and gosling, wild horse and foal. The babies are round, fluffy and wide-eyed. An Orbi carries a sleepy baby gently in its field. Reunions show hugs, nuzzles and wing wraps, never sad crying.

### Expanded play

Families grow from two choices to three and four, and new homes appear, a nest, a burrow, a pond and a tree hollow, so the child can bring each family home. A sound mode lets a baby call and its mama answer. A goodnight scene shows every family asleep together.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Animal families, baby animal names**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69): tap before drag, no reading, no failure, co-play under age 2.
