## Story

"Why is the tower so dim tonight?" asked Trip. "Turtles," said Doubl. "Bright lights confuse the babies. They need the moonlight to find the sea." They watched Pim rise, then Pom. The two moons slid closer and closer until one covered the other, and the beach went darker for a moment. The tide crept up to their toes. Then, out of the sand, tiny turtles ran toward the silver water. Nobody cheered out loud. Doubl just squeezed Trip's hand. Some nights the kindest light is the one you turn down.

> **Wisdom:** Sometimes the kindest light is a dim one.

### Gameplay

A big time wheel at the bottom turns the 26-hour day; above a quiet island beach, the two small moons Pim and Pom slide into a double eclipse while the tide rises and a tower crown dims for nesting turtles.

### World

A quiet island coast at night under a deep, dark sky full of stars and a bright band of the Milky Way. A tall white tower stands on the flat dunes, its crown shielded and softly glowing. Pim and Pom cast double shadows on the sand. Tide pools shine, seabirds sleep on the rocks, and fireflies drift over the dune grass.

### Characters and materials

Doubl and Trip sit on a dune in white lattice suits that glow faintly at the wrists, coral red and sky blue, while their Orbis float low and dim. The two small moons look a little different: Pim pearly and round, Pom rosy and slightly smaller. Night palette: navy, silver, soft teal tide water, warm low tower glow. No lamps anywhere.

### Expanded play

New nights unlock seasons and events: a spring tide when both moons line up, a turtle nesting night when the tower turns its light lower still, a meteor shower, and a sunrise when the 26 hours begin again. A journal mode asks the player to predict the next eclipse and the next high tide, then watch it happen.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Day and night, moon phases, tides, eclipses, dark skies for animals**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Check every detail against the [Raya canon](../../lore/raya.md): untouched nature, white towers and clothing, Orbis, the Guard and the Gentle Defaults.
