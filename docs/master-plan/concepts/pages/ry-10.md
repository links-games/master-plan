## Story

Trip wanted a picture of the heron. He crept closer, and the heron lifted one foot, ready to fly. "Stop here," said his Orbi. "Let her decide." So Trip sat on a log in the mud and waited. The tide came in. A crab walked over his boot. The heron forgot all about him and, all at once, dipped into the water and came up with a silver fish. Click. Later Trip sketched it slowly, every feather. His best picture was the one he waited for, not the one he chased.

> **Wisdom:** Wait, and nature comes to you.

### Gameplay

Through a white rounded viewfinder, a mangrove delta fills the frame: a heron stands still in shallow water near the feet of a slender white tower, and a soft guide ring turns green when the shot is calm and well framed.

### World

A mangrove delta in golden morning light, with tangled roots, still channels and drifting mist. A white Chord tower stands in shallow water on three root-like feet, its reflection long and wobbly. Herons, crabs and small fish share the scene, and a distant line of islands sits on a clear horizon. Quiet, patient, painterly mood.

### Characters and materials

Trip, in a white lattice field suit with green accents and soft gray mud patches on the knees, kneels on a fallen log while his Orbi hovers low to show a framing ring. The sketch page is cream paper with charcoal lines and watercolor washes. Animals are true to life but friendly. Palette: muddy greens, heron gray, tower white, sunrise peach.

### Expanded play

New places fill the journal: a red rock canyon at dusk, coral shallows seen from a floating nest, a flower steppe with migrating herds, a tower crown under the Milky Way. A sketch mode turns each photo into a traced drawing, and the best pages come from waiting until animals act naturally, never from getting closer or using a flash.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Observation, patience, composition, animal behavior**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Check every detail against the [Raya canon](../../lore/raya.md): untouched nature, white towers and clothing, Orbis, the Guard and the Gentle Defaults.
