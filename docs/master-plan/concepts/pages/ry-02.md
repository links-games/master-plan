# One Night Apple

RY-02 · [Raya life](../ry.md) · Cx 1 · Ages 3–5 · Added for the Raya feedback

**The idea:** An old apple tree fills the screen at dawn; a white Orbi hovers beside one red apple and wraps it in soft rings of pale blue scanning light, while a big glowing tap circle invites the next step.

| Decision             | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familiar mechanic    | Step-by-step story toy (ask, scan, wait, enjoy)                                                                                                                                             |
| Learning objective   | Copy, don't take; patience; caring for birds                                                                                                                                                |
| First prototype      | One authored challenge built around the interaction above, with a clear goal, immediate feedback, restart and unlimited retries.                                                            |
| Technology           | React + SVG/DOM; deterministic state, undo and seeded levels. Start with one local screen and touch plus keyboard input.                                                                    |
| Scope boundary       | One board and one rule set; defer accounts, multiplayer, procedural content and platform SDKs.                                                                                              |
| Reuse                | Shared input, saves, accessibility, seeded state and the raya life collection shell.                                                                                                        |
| Prototype acceptance | A player can identify the next action from the screen, complete one challenge, explain the practiced idea and restart without help. Check touch and keyboard play at narrow and wide sizes. |
| Main uncertainty     | Test whether the lesson follows from play and whether the first interaction is understandable without reading.                                                                              |

## Story

Mira's little cousin wanted the red apple. "Ask first," said the Orbi. They looked. A bird was singing in the tree, but the apple was far from her nest. "Now hold still," said the Orbi, and it wrapped the apple in soft blue light. The apple never left its branch. "When can I eat it?" "Tomorrow. Tonight the garden will make one." All night the assembler hummed. In the morning a shiny red apple lay on a leaf. And the first apple? It stayed on the tree, for the bird.

> **Wisdom:** Copy, don't take. Leave the tree whole.

### Gameplay

An old apple tree fills the screen at dawn; a white Orbi hovers beside one red apple and wraps it in soft rings of pale blue scanning light, while a big glowing tap circle invites the next step.

### World

The apple tree at dawn on a gentle hill, its branches wide and mossy, with a small bird nest tucked into one fork. Behind it lies a misty valley of untouched forest and one tall white Chord tower glowing faintly at its crown. A white lattice nest sits in a nearby tree, and a garden assembler waits quietly among wild herbs below it.

### Characters and materials

A white Orbi sphere, 30 cm across, with a coral-red left eye and a sky-blue right eye, sends thin rings of pale blue light around the apple without touching it. The apple stays on its branch. The garden assembler is a smooth white pod with a soft moonlit glow. Colors: apple red, leaf green, dawn gold, pearly white. Soft rounded shapes, no text.

### Expanded play

More trees join over time: a pear, a plum bush, a berry vine and a nut tree, each with its own animal to check on first, such as a squirrel or a bee. A night scene shows the moons Pim and Pom over the nest while the assembler glows, and a morning scene shows the copied fruit laid out on big leaves.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Copy, don't take; patience; caring for birds**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Check every detail against the [Raya canon](../../lore/raya.md): untouched nature, white towers and clothing, Orbis, the Guard and the Gentle Defaults.
