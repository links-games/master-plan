# Share Basket

RY-12 · [Raya life](../ry.md) · Cx 1 · Ages 3–5 · Added for the Raya feedback

**The idea:** A white Orbi floats a woven basket through a wild-looking garden; the child taps ripe berries and beans to collect them, then drags one to each of three neighbor nest baskets waiting on low branches.

| Decision             | Proposal                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Familiar mechanic    | Gather, count and share                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Learning objective   | Sharing fairly, counting to five, taking only what animals do not need                                                                                                                      |
| First prototype      | One authored challenge built around the interaction above, with a clear goal, immediate feedback, restart and unlimited retries.                                                            |
| Technology           | React + SVG/DOM; deterministic state, undo and seeded levels. Start with one local screen and touch plus keyboard input.                                                                    |
| Scope boundary       | One board and one rule set; defer accounts, multiplayer, procedural content and platform SDKs.                                                                                              |
| Reuse                | Shared input, saves, accessibility, seeded state and the raya life collection shell.                                                                                                        |
| Prototype acceptance | A player can identify the next action from the screen, complete one challenge, explain the practiced idea and restart without help. Check touch and keyboard play at narrow and wide sizes. |
| Main uncertainty     | Test whether the lesson follows from play and whether the first interaction is understandable without reading.                                                                              |

## Estimated plan

**First playable version:** 8 garden rounds where the child taps fruit into the basket, then shares one by one into two to five nest baskets, with one bush always left for the animals. Picnic and night copy scenes come later.

## Story

The basket was full of blueberries. "Who gets them?" asked the Orbi. The little one looked at the nests. One, two, three neighbors. One berry for you. One for you. One for you. Again and again, until the plates looked the same. "And the bush by the river?" The little one shook her head. "That one is for the birds." That night the assembler hummed, and in the morning there were more berries, enough for a picnic. Everyone came. Everyone brought something. Nobody had to ask for more.

> **Wisdom:** Share what you have, and there is enough.

### Gameplay

A white Orbi floats a woven basket through a wild-looking garden; the child taps ripe berries and beans to collect them, then drags one to each of three neighbor nest baskets waiting on low branches.

### World

A river beach of round pebbles beside a slender white Chord tower, with children's white lattice nests in the trees and a wild-looking garden of berry bushes, bean vines and little pumpkins growing among ferns. A garden assembler, a smooth white pod, rests under a tree. Soft afternoon light, butterflies, and a small bird among the berries.

### Characters and materials

A white Orbi with a coral-red left eye and a sky-blue right eye carries a basket of white lattice in its soft field. The food is big and round: blueberries, beans, apples, little pumpkins. Neighbor nests show only a round door and a basket. A small bird and a hedgehog-like animal have their own share left on the bush. Bright, simple shapes.

### Expanded play

Later baskets grow: four and five neighbors, sharing so that every plate looks the same, a night when the assembler copies berries while Pim and Pom glow, and a picnic where everyone brings something different. One gentle rule stays in every level: one bush is always left for the animals, and the child taps it to watch them feast.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Sharing fairly, counting to five, taking only what animals do not need**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Check every detail against the [Raya canon](../../lore/raya.md): untouched nature, white towers and clothing, Orbis, the Guard and the Gentle Defaults.
