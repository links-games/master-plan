**The idea:** A picture of a duck is split into two huge pieces; one half waits inside its glowing outline while the child drags the other half close, and it snaps into place with a happy pop.

## Story

Oh no, the picture of the duck was in two parts! One part had the head. One part had the tail. Linky picked up the head and slid it, slowly, slowly, next to the tail. Click! The duck was whole again. Quack! said the duck, and it swam away across the stone. Then Linky found a picture of the fawn in three parts. Click, click, click. When parts come together, something new can happen.

> **Wisdom:** Little parts make a whole.

### Gameplay

A picture of a duck is split into two huge pieces; one half waits inside its glowing outline while the child drags the other half close, and it snaps into place with a happy pop.

### World

A flat sunny stone in the clover meadow works as the puzzle table, with the short white Chord tower and grazing fawns softly blurred behind it. Even light and no harsh shadows. The empty spot for each piece glows faintly, like a footprint in the grass, so the target is easy to see.

### Characters and materials

Pieces are thick rounded shapes like wooden toy puzzle pieces, cut straight into halves, thirds and quarters, never with tricky tabs. Pictures show a duck, a fawn, Linky, an Orbi, the sun and a sailboat. Snap areas are big and forgiving. Warm wood edges, bright flat colors and soft gray outlines.

### Expanded play

Puzzles grow from two pieces to three and then four, and every finished picture comes alive: the duck quacks and swims away, the boat sails off. Sets cover animals, friends and toys. A finished-puzzle meadow shows every picture the child has built, all waving hello.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Parts and wholes, shapes, hand-eye coordination**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69): tap before drag, no reading, no failure, co-play under age 2.
