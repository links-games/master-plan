## Story

The valley wanted a festival at the next double eclipse. But the herd in the side valley was calving, and Fennel, who lived without an Orbi, said loud songs kept her awake. Nobody voted. They listened. The children wanted music. Professor Acorn wanted to watch the calves. At last Fennel said, "What if the song plays on the far ridge, after the calving season ends?" One by one every glow turned green, and Fennel nodded too. The new default: festival after calving, on the far ridge. Anyone could stay home. Nobody had to come.

> **Wisdom:** Listen until everyone can say yes.

### Gameplay

Citizens sit in a circle of flat stones, each with a picture card of their need floating above them, and the player drags proposal pieces into a shared glowing default until every Orbi in the circle glows calm green.

### World

A ridge meadow overlooking a valley of towers that fades into blue haze, in cool late-morning light. A ring of flat stones sits in the grass with nothing built around it. Below, a river winds through untouched forest, a herd of grazers rests with new calves in a quiet side valley, and three slender white towers stand far apart, pale against the sky.

### Characters and materials

Valley people of every kind: young-looking adults in white lattice suits with colored accents, children in looser cuts, Professor Acorn in his leaf-patterned white suit, and Fennel, a quiet woman with no Orbi who speaks aloud. Orbis float at shoulder height, glowing amber when unsure and green when content. Need cards are picture tiles: a calf, a song, a light, sleep.

### Expanded play

Later topics grow harder: when the festival song may play, how many copies the night assemblers should make in a long dry spell, quiet trails during calving, and how to include people who live off the Chord. Each agreed default joins a woven valley charter, where anyone may opt out for themselves, but never in a way that harms others.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Listening, compromise, consensus and optional rules**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Check every detail against the [Raya canon](../../lore/raya.md): untouched nature, white towers and clothing, Orbis, the Guard and the Gentle Defaults.
