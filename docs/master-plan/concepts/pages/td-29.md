## Story

One fawn came to the pond. Hello, one! Then another fawn came. One, two! Two fawns. Then a tiny fawn came running and slipped in the clover. Oops! One, two, three. Three fawns! The Beadle of Three wiggled its three beads. Me too! Linky counted on its fingers. One, two, three. Then the fawns ran off to play, one, two, three, and Linky waved three times.

> **Wisdom:** Count with me: one, two, three.

### Gameplay

Three fawns stand in a row in the clover; the child taps each fawn, it hops, and a warm voice counts one, two, three as three glowing dots light up in the sky.

### World

A wide, gentle meadow in the morning with the short white Chord tower, a pond and an apple tree. Things come in small groups everywhere: two ducks, three flowers, one cloud. Clear light and empty space between the groups make every amount easy to see at a glance.

### Characters and materials

Fawns, ducks, apples, bubbles and Beadles, the bead-chain friends, where the Beadle of Three has three beads. Objects are big and spaced apart. Amounts are shown with glowing dots, never written numerals. Linky holds up fingers on its red and blue hands. A soft pastel palette.

### Expanded play

Counting grows from one and two to three, then to choosing which of two baskets holds three apples. A feed-the-friends mode asks the child to give two berries to a fawn. Later, groups go up to five. Every count is voiced together, and a miscount is simply counted again together.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Counting to three, small amounts**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69): tap before drag, no reading, no failure, co-play under age 2.
