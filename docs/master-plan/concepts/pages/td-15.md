**The idea:** A muddy fawn stands in a round wooden tub of warm water; the child rubs a big foamy cloud sponge over the brown mud spots, and they vanish under clouds of rainbow bubbles.

## Story

The fawn played in the mud all day. Splish, splosh! Now its legs were brown, its nose was brown, and even one ear was brown. Linky filled the tub with warm water. The Orbi made it just right, not too hot. Linky took a soft sponge and rubbed the mud away. Scrub, scrub, bubbles everywhere! Then came a splash of clean water and a big fluffy towel. The fawn shook itself and sprinkled everyone. Clean and cozy feels good.

> **Wisdom:** Clean and cozy feels good.

### Gameplay

A muddy fawn stands in a round wooden tub of warm water; the child rubs a big foamy cloud sponge over the brown mud spots, and they vanish under clouds of rainbow bubbles.

### World

A sunny bath corner beside a little stream in the clover meadow, with a round wooden tub, smooth stones and a towel hanging on a white lattice frame. The short white Chord tower shines in the background. Steam curls softly above the water. Warm, clean and cheerful light.

### Characters and materials

The fawn has soft brown mud spots that shine when clean. A sponge shaped like a cloud, soap that makes rainbow bubbles, a watering can for rinsing and a fluffy towel. Linky helps with its red and blue hands, and an Orbi warms the water with a gentle glow. Foam, splash and soft cloth textures.

### Expanded play

Later, other friends need a wash too: Linky, sticky after painting, a sandy duck and Verbi covered in leaves. New bath toys appear, a floating boat and a squirting frog. After the rinse comes the towel and a cozy shake, and the last bath of the day ends with pajamas and a yawn.

## Design checks before prototyping

- Learning target: **Washing, body care, cause and effect**. Ask a domain reviewer to validate factual content in the mechanic.
- Keep goals cooperative or self-directed, using resources and needs. Avoid purchasable power. No pressure timers in the accessible mode.
- Use original layouts and character designs; the [legal checklist](../../legal.md) applies even when a mechanic is familiar.
- Choose the minimum [fidelity level](../../simulation-engine.md#determinism-and-fidelity-levels) the lesson needs; concept art does not establish scientific accuracy.
- Review silhouettes, text alternatives, reduced motion and non-color cues against the [adorability gate](../../lore/adorability-gate.md).
- Follow the [toddler design rules](../../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#7-toddlers-and-screens-r68-r69): tap before drag, no reading, no failure, co-play under age 2.
