# Character Bible — the Links

Covers requirements **R13, R14, R15, R16, R30**. World rules, palette and
tone are in the [lore core](README.md); every design passes the
[adorability gate](adorability-gate.md). The research behind each
character (sources, quotes, benchmarks) is in
[research-links-theory-lore.md](../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-links-theory-lore.md)
sections 5–9.

All names are **working names** until trademark searches finish
([legal](../legal.md#names-and-trademarks)).

## Design principle

Each character **is** a concept of Links Theory, the way each Numberblock
_is_ a number. The body shows the structure of the concept; the behavior
teaches a lesson that is true both in the theory and in life. Toddlers see
a cute friend, children learn the lesson, adults recognize the theory.

## Style sheets

Every character sheet in `links-games/assets` contains all five styles
from the [lore core](README.md#styles-r30). Production notes per style:

### Chibi 2D (primary)

- Head-to-body 1:1 to 1:2; shapes by role (round, square, triangle).
- Large simple eyes from the avatar's filled circles, one highlight.
  Mouths minimal or absent at rest, so viewers project their own feelings.
- Thick rounded `#4D4D4D` outlines, flat fills with at most one shadow
  tone.
- Source files: SVG + [Rive](https://rive.app) state machines
  (`idle`, `happy`, `curious`, `sleepy`, `surprised`, `oops`, `proud`,
  `comforted`, `link`).
- Sticker and pixel variants at 512, 128, 48 and 16 px; the 48 px variant
  is also the organization avatar candidate.

### Normal anime 2D

- Hosts at 1:6–1:7, soft cel shading, the same palette.
- Link creatures stay chibi next to them.
- Links are glowing threads: red-to-blue gradient threads are friendships,
  green knots are actions.

### 3D

- **Creatures:** toy-like vinyl or felt material, rim light, rounded
  bevels, squash and stretch. Built from a few primitives (sphere, rounded
  cube, cone) so they are cheap on mobile and can be generated
  procedurally; the same shapes become plush and keychain patterns.
- **Hosts:** toon shader with outlines.
- **Links:** bezier ropes with light physics.
- Format: glTF 2.0 (`.glb`) for three.js / React Three Fiber.

### Consistency rules (all styles)

1. The red/blue eyes or red/blue hands are always present on Link
   characters.
2. Green appears whenever an action happens.
3. The infinity loop is reserved for Itsy, Akoren and Omi.
4. Every design is recognizable as a pure black silhouette.

## Age tiers

The same characters, tuned per tier (tiers are a `game-core` setting, see
[infrastructure](../infrastructure.md)):

| Tier             | Ages | What changes                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Toddlers         | 2–5  | Creatures only, no text (icons and voice), no timers, no fail states, parental gate on everything outside play. |
| Children         | 6–12 | Hosts appear; short text with voice-over; gentle challenges; lesson cards.                                      |
| Teens and adults | 13+  | Full puzzles, theory Easter eggs, LiNo snippets, optional leaderboards, adult jokes in the second layer.        |

## Adding a new character

1. Open an issue with the concept it **is** (from Links Theory, LiNo or
   the Links data constants), the lesson, and the first game.
2. Propose a chibi sketch that follows the consistency rules.
3. Run the [adorability gate](adorability-gate.md) with five people from
   three age groups and the theory consistency check.
4. Add the sheet and the LiNo entry to `links-games/lore` and the assets to
   `links-games/assets`.
