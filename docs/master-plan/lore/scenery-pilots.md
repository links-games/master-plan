# Scenery pilots

Short pilot-episode ideas for the two worlds of the Links universe: [Raya](raya.md), the home world of untouched nature and white Chord towers, [Shacados](shacados.md), the rusted world, and the moment they meet in [First Contact](first-contact.md). Each pilot is a self-contained scene. It can become an animated short, a loading-screen series or the opening of a game. The maintainer asked for "ideas on different scenery pilots, so we later will be able to picture them" (requirements R55–R76 in the [case study](../../case-studies/issue-1/requirements.md#raya-shacados-toddlers-and-the-website-r55r76)). All 22 pilot settings now have [Blender environment studies](scenes/README.md), native rendered backgrounds and low-detail interactive versions on the website. The image descriptions remain briefs for the characters and story shots; the environment renders do not claim to implement the animated episodes.

Status: **draft for maintainer review.** Names, places and numbers that the lore does not already state are marked _(proposal)_ and collected in [open questions](#open-questions). Read-aloud stories set in the same places are in [stories with wisdom](stories.md).

## How to use these pilots

Every scene has the same card: a logline, the setting, beats, a wisdom line, an image description, sound and mood, ages, and the games it can seed.

- **As an animated short.** The beats are the shot list. Each pilot runs 60–120 seconds, with no dialogue that a picture can say instead.
- **As a loading-screen series.** The image description is the key frame. Beats 1, 3 and the last beat give two more variants. The wisdom line is the caption, added by the game as translated UI text, never painted into the image.
- **As a game opening.** Play the beats as a 20–40 second intro, then hand control to the player in one of the games under "Seeds games".
- **Image prompts.** Copy the paragraph as it is and add the shared negative prompt: "no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures, no UI". Raya frames follow the [background rules](raya.md#loading-screens-and-backgrounds): nature fills at least 70% of the frame, towers are white, thin and few, and there are no roads, fences, power lines or crowds. Shacados frames follow the [visual guide](shacados.md#visual-guide): the rust, soot, mustard, ash and sick-green palette, one warm lamp-orange light, one small living green thing, tired faces with dignity, and never gore, corpses, weapons in use or cruelty as spectacle.
- **Ages.** Labels follow the [age tiers](characters.md#age-tiers). 1+ means co-play with a parent. Toddler scenes show only creatures, Orbis and Raya's meadows. Children never see Shacados threats. Shacados and First Contact scenes are 10+ when they show only repair, sharing and hope, and 13+ otherwise.
- **Physics checklist.** Raya may go beyond today's science only through lore exceptions E1–E5 in the [research note](../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md). Chord flight needs a lattice flight suit, works only within about 2 km of a tower and moves at 5–40 km/h. An Orbi lifts a few kilograms within about 1 m. A copy takes one night. The day has 26 hours, the year has 401 days, and the two small moons are Pim and Pom. Shacados has no magical exceptions. Its extreme spaceflight proposals still require feasibility work; a calculated upper bound is not proof of buildable hardware.
- **Quality.** Every scene passes the [adorability gate](adorability-gate.md) for its tier and the Shacados tone rule: dark but never hopeless, people never villains, the player never harms.

## Raya scenes

### 1. The clover meadow

**Logline:** A fawn, a floating dew drop and a white sphere say hello to the smallest viewer.

**Setting:** The toddler home place: a soft clover meadow at the foot of a short white tower, about 300 m tall, on a low ridge. Late morning in the long green spring. High, soft sunlight, a few round clouds, a light breeze. Physics note: the "floating bubbles" of this home place are dew drops that the Orbi holds still in the air with its field, within a meter of itself. Acoustic levitation of water drops is real.

**Beats:**

1. A wide, quiet meadow. A spotted fawn steps out of tall grass.
2. A white Orbi floats in at the height of a small child's eyes. Its eyes appear, coral red on the left and sky blue on the right, and blink "hello".
3. The Orbi lifts one dew drop from a clover leaf. The drop hangs in the air, shining, with a tiny rainbow inside.
4. The fawn sniffs the drop. Plip! It lands on the fawn's nose. The fawn hops, surprised. The Orbi's eyes curve into a smile.
5. The fawn lies down in the clover. The Orbi dims its light and rests beside it. The scene ends calm, with no cliffhanger.

**Wisdom:** "Everyone is welcome." — Anya

**Image description:** Low, child's-eye-level wide shot of a soft clover meadow in late morning, hand-painted anime background style with gentle watercolor textures. In the foreground, a spotted fawn with big dark eyes sniffs a single shining dew drop that floats in the air. Beside it hovers a smooth white sphere about 30 cm across, with a soft glowing ring around its middle and two round glowing eyes, coral red on the left and sky blue on the right, curved in a smile. Far behind, on a green ridge, stands one slender white tower, slightly twisted like a plant stem, with ring-shaped nodes like bamboo. Pale blue sky, round white clouds, tiny white and pink clover flowers. Palette of soft greens, cream white, coral red and sky blue. Warm, safe, sleepy mood. No people.

**Sound and mood:** Wind in the grass, a far bee, one soft two-note "boop-bip" and a tiny "plip"; lullaby tempo.

**Ages:** 1+ (co-play)

**Seeds games:** [Bubble Canopy](../concepts/pages/pz-20.md), [Pair Friends](../concepts/pages/pz-01.md), [Bird Watch](../concepts/pages/nc-04.md)

### 2. The apple at dawn

**Logline:** Mira wants an apple, and her Orbi Pip shows her how to have it without taking it.

**Setting:** The "Raya life" home place: an old wild apple tree at the edge of a blue-green forest, below Mira's nest. Dawn in early summer. Low golden light through mist, dew on every leaf. Physics note: Pip holds the apple still with a diamagnetic field, scans it through its skin in a few seconds, and never moves it from the branch. The garden assembler builds the copy overnight from air, water and compost, using tower energy (lore exception E3).

**Beats:**

1. Mira, 9, climbs down from her nest in the tree crown to the meadow, with Pip floating beside her.
2. Mira reaches for a red apple. Pip says, "Ask first." She looks, and finds a nest with three blue eggs on the next branch.
3. Pip rises to the apple. A thin ring of light passes through it from top to bottom. The apple does not swing, and no leaf falls.
4. Night. Pim and Pom hang over the trees. In the nest, the garden assembler hums, and a new apple slowly takes shape in a cradle of white lattice.
5. Morning. Mira bites her copy. On the tree, the original apple still hangs, and a small bird pecks it.

**Wisdom:** "Copy, don't take." — Orbi

**Image description:** Medium shot at dawn under an old wild apple tree, hand-painted anime style, soft volumetric light through morning mist. A girl of nine in a tight white lattice suit with a coral-red accent at her left wrist and a sky-blue accent at her right wrist stands on dewy grass, looking up. Above her, a white sphere companion 30 cm across hovers beside one red apple, and a thin ring of pale light passes through the apple without touching it. On the next branch, a small woven bird nest with three blue eggs. Behind, a blue-green forest fades into mist, and far away one slender white tower catches the first gold light. Palette of gold, mint green, white and apple red. Gentle, curious, respectful mood.

**Sound and mood:** Birdsong, dripping dew, a soft rising hum while Pip scans; morning-quiet and warm.

**Ages:** 3+

**Seeds games:** [Garden Grow](../concepts/pages/nc-09.md), [Harvest Drop](../concepts/pages/pz-23.md), [Bird Watch](../concepts/pages/nc-04.md)

### 3. First ride on the Chord

**Logline:** Mira's class learns to ride the Chord, and learns where the Chord ends.

**Setting:** A river valley around a 700 m tower _(proposal)_. Mid-afternoon in summer, clear sky, a light breeze, a river full of salmon-like fish. Physics note: Chord flight works only within about 2 km of a tower, at walking to cycling speed, and only in a flight suit whose pearl panels catch the tower field (lore exception E1). The lesson is a loop along the river that never leaves the 2 km ring.

**Beats:**

1. The teacher, who looks 20 and is 1,400 years old, checks each flight suit. Pearl panels on backs, arms and shins glow faintly.
2. The children rise slowly. Feet leave the grass. Someone squeals, then laughs.
3. They glide in a line along the river at the speed of a bicycle. Fish flash below. The Orbis fly beside them like white balloons.
4. At the edge of the ring, the field fades. The teacher lets Mira feel it: she sinks softly, a little, then turns back. "The tower can only reach so far."
5. They land on a warm pebble beach. Mira's legs wobble. She lies on the stones and grins at the sky.

**Wisdom:** "Big things are made of small steps." — Choo-Choo Seq

**Image description:** Wide aerial shot above a clear turquoise river winding through a green valley, painterly anime style, bright afternoon light. A line of five children in tight white flight suits with pearl-colored panels on their backs, arms and shins glide one meter above the water, arms spread, at cycling speed. White sphere companions with coral-red and sky-blue eyes float beside them. A young adult teacher in a white suit with a green collar leads the line. Silver fish shapes under the water. On the left, close, a slender white tower rises 700 m, slightly twisted, with bamboo-like rings, and its crown disappears into a thin cloud. Forests and mountains fade into blue haze. Palette of turquoise, emerald, white and pearl. Joyful, free, safe mood.

**Sound and mood:** Wind, the river, children's laughter, a faint high shimmer for the field; joyful and airy.

**Ages:** 6+

**Seeds games:** [Sound Waves](../concepts/pages/sl-13.md), [Kite Day](../concepts/pages/sm-08.md), [Paper Planes](../concepts/pages/sm-07.md)

### 4. The double eclipse

**Logline:** Pim and Pom bite two holes in the sun, and the forest floor fills with tiny crescents.

**Setting:** The puzzles home place: a fern garden with old stone circles under a twisted white spire. Midday in summer, clear sky. Physics note _(proposal)_: Pim and Pom are too small to cover the whole star, so a double eclipse is a double partial eclipse. The light dims to silver, the air cools a little, and birds fall quiet. Gaps between leaves work as pinhole cameras and paint the star's shape on the ground: crescents with two bites. Nobody looks at the star directly; the Orbis show a filtered image.

**Beats:**

1. Professor Acorn, Lina, Doubl and Trip sit in a stone circle. The Orbis project a dimmed image of the star onto a white lattice sheet.
2. Pim's dark disk touches the star. Some minutes later, Pom touches the other side.
3. The light turns silver. Shadows grow sharp. The birds stop singing.
4. Under the ferns, hundreds of little suns with two bites dance on the ground. Trip traces one with a finger.
5. The light comes back. One bird sings first. Acorn says, "The moons did not take the light. They only rested in front of it."

**Wisdom:** "Rest is important. Nothing is also something." — Nully

**Image description:** Ground-level shot in a fern garden at midday during a partial eclipse, hand-painted anime style, unusual silver-blue light with sharp shadows. On mossy ground between giant ferns, hundreds of small bright crescent-shaped light spots, each with two curved bites, fall through the gaps in the leaves. A boy of seven in a white lattice suit with green accents kneels and touches one crescent with his finger. Behind him, an old man with round spectacles shaped like an infinity loop, in a fitted white suit with a leaf pattern, smiles. Ancient stone circles stand among the ferns. Through the canopy, a slender white spire rises against a dim blue sky. White sphere companions glow softly. Palette of silver, fern green, white and pale gold. Hushed, wondering mood.

**Sound and mood:** Birdsong fading to silence, leaves rustling, one bird singing again at the end; hushed wonder.

**Ages:** 3+

**Seeds games:** [Orbit Garden](../concepts/pages/sp-05.md), [Telescope Tour](../concepts/pages/sp-09.md), [Light and Color](../concepts/pages/sl-01.md)

### 5. The arm that stopped

**Logline:** Theo tries to push Mira into the river as a joke, and the Guard holds his arm.

**Setting:** The preschool home place: a river beach of round pebbles beside a tower. Late afternoon, warm gold light, high summer. Physics note: the Guard pauses a motor command for a moment when a movement would hurt someone. It does not move Theo, and it never touches what he thinks or wants. Theo has chosen to keep his Guard on; nobody can waive it for Mira. Mira is standing on rocks, so a surprise push could hurt her.

**Beats:**

1. Mira stands on a rock at the water's edge, watching fish.
2. Theo sneaks up behind her, grinning, hands out.
3. His arms stop in the air, as if they hit a soft invisible pillow. His Orbi blinks calmly. Theo wobbles and sits down on the pebbles.
4. Mira turns around. Both burst out laughing. Theo's ears go red.
5. A thin thread of light links their two Orbis: Mira speaks through the Chord. "Next time ask, and I'll push you back." Theo asks. Mira points: "Into the deep pool, not onto the rocks." This time the push is gentle and the water is deep. Splash.

**Wisdom:** "Ask first." — Orbi

**Image description:** Medium shot on a river beach of round, sun-warmed pebbles in late-afternoon gold light, painterly anime style. A boy of nine in a white lattice suit sits on the pebbles with a surprised, embarrassed grin, his arms still half raised. A girl of nine in a white suit with coral-red and sky-blue wrist accents stands on a flat rock at the water's edge and laughs. Two white sphere companions float between them, their coral-red and sky-blue eyes calm, and a thin glowing thread of light connects the two spheres. Clear shallow water with silver fish, a deep green pool behind, children's nests woven into the trees on the far bank, and one slender white tower beyond. Palette of warm gold, river green, white and soft coral. Playful, forgiving mood.

**Sound and mood:** River babble, a soft "hold" chime, laughter, a big splash at the end; playful.

**Ages:** 6+

**Seeds games:** [Kindness Quests](../concepts/pages/sa-02.md), [Crossing Friends](../concepts/pages/sm-15.md)

### 6. A tower grows

**Logline:** One Rayan year of growing becomes one minute, as assemblers raise a white tower from stone and air.

**Setting:** A bare granite ridge where the Chord has a gap. A time-lapse over one full year of 401 days: the long green spring, summer, a short gold autumn and a snowy winter. Physics note: a tower is grown layer by layer by assemblers from local stone and air, without roads or cranes, and takes about a year. Its three root-like feet stand on bedrock about 30 m across in total. Swarms of fist-sized white assemblers are a _(proposal)_.

**Beats:**

1. A small group of Rayans with Orbis walks the ridge. They mark three points on the rock, and find a marmot burrow under one of them. They move that foot 5 m.
2. Fist-sized white assemblers settle on the marks like bees. White stone grows out of the rock like roots.
3. Time-lapse: the spire rises like a bamboo shoot. Rings of emitters appear like nodes. Behind it, flowers bloom, grass goes gold, and snow falls and melts.
4. Day 401. At dusk, the crown lights softly for the first time. Thirty kilometers away, the next tower answers with one blink of light.
5. Morning. The marmot sits on one of the white feet, warming itself in the sun.

**Wisdom:** "Big trees grow from tiny acorns." — Akoren

**Image description:** Wide, low-angle shot of a granite mountain ridge at dusk, painterly anime style with clean shapes. A new, slender white ceramic tower rises from three root-like feet gripping the bare rock, slightly twisted like a plant stem, with ring-shaped emitter nodes like bamboo. Its crown glows softly for the first time. Far away on the horizon, a second tiny white tower answers with a small point of light. Near the base, a marmot sits on one smooth white foot. Small groups of fist-sized white machines rest on the stone like sleeping bees. Alpine flowers grow undisturbed around the feet. The sky fades from apricot to deep blue, with the first stars. Palette of granite gray, apricot, deep blue and white. Patient, proud mood.

**Sound and mood:** Wind on rock, a soft rising chord as the time-lapse runs, silence when the crown lights; patient and proud.

**Ages:** 6+

**Seeds games:** [Tiny Architect](../concepts/pages/bd-10.md), [Bridge Builder](../concepts/pages/sl-15.md), [Satellite Network](../concepts/pages/sp-11.md)

### 7. The tower song

**Logline:** Once a year the towers sing aloud, and a whole steppe listens.

**Setting:** The music home place: a festival on a flower steppe. Dusk turning to night, late summer, after the breeding season, warm and still. Physics note: tower emitters usually work in ultrasound, above human hearing. The audible song plays only at festivals and never in breeding seasons. Sound takes about 90 seconds to cross 30 km, so the song is written as a round _(proposal)_: each tower's answer arrives exactly when the melody needs it.

**Beats:**

1. People arrive walking and gliding, in fitted white festival suits with glowing trim. A herd of grazers watches from a distance.
2. The near tower plays one low note. The grass trembles.
3. A minute and a half later, the far tower's answer rolls in over the steppe and fits the melody perfectly.
4. Children dance in circles. Orbis glow in rhythm. Someone plays a reed flute in harmony.
5. Night. The tower lights dim for the animals. The last note fades, and Pim rises over the grass.

**Wisdom:** "A link is a beginning and an end holding hands." — Soru & Tari

**Image description:** Very wide shot of a flower steppe at dusk, painterly anime style, warm violet and rose sky. In the middle ground, dozens of small figures in tight white festival suits with softly glowing trim dance in loose circles among tall grasses and wildflowers. Many white sphere companions glow softly above them like lanterns. A slender white tower stands close on the left, its ring nodes glowing faintly. A second white tower stands tiny on the far horizon. A herd of deer-like grazers watches calmly from the right. A small pale moon rises low over the grass. Palette of violet, rose, wheat gold and pearl white. No crowds pressing together, lots of open space. Festive, gentle, spacious mood.

**Sound and mood:** One deep tower note, a delayed answering note, a reed flute, crickets; festive and spacious.

**Ages:** 3+

**Seeds games:** [Melody Garden](../concepts/pages/mr-06.md), [Drum Circle](../concepts/pages/mr-02.md), [Echo Links](../concepts/pages/mr-01.md)

### 8. Rain in the delta

**Logline:** Lina and Trip count young herons in a warm rain, and learn to watch before they help.

**Setting:** The nature and care home place: a mangrove delta with herons, where a tower stands in shallow water. A warm afternoon in the rainy season; a rain wall crosses the water, then a rainbow. Physics note: lattice closes into a rain shell. Rayans drink rain, never river water, because rivers belong to the rivers. They glide low and slowly, inside the tower's 2 km ring.

**Beats:**

1. Lina and Trip glide low over a mangrove channel, slowly, so the birds stay calm.
2. The rain arrives like a gray curtain. Their suits close into shells with soft hoods.
3. They hover still at a distance. Their Orbis show a close picture of a heron nest. Trip counts three chicks.
4. One chick has climbed out onto a root. Trip wants to lift it back. Lina shakes her head: "Watch first." The chick climbs back by itself, and the parent lands beside it.
5. The rain stops. A rainbow arches over the tower. They fill a gourd with rain from a leaf and share it.

**Wisdom:** "Do no harm — to others, to yourself, to nature." — the Gentle Defaults

**Image description:** Eye-level shot across a mangrove channel just after warm rain, painterly anime style, wet glossy leaves, soft green-gray light breaking into sunshine. Two young people in tight white suits closed into smooth rain shells with soft hoods hover low above the water, keeping their distance. One has red and blue hair clips and a green ring necklace; the younger one has green accents. Two white sphere companions float beside them with coral-red and sky-blue eyes. On the mangrove roots ahead, a gray heron stands beside a nest with three fluffy chicks. Behind them, a slender white tower stands in the shallow water under a rainbow. Raindrops ripple the surface. Palette of mangrove green, wet silver, white and rainbow tints. Patient, careful mood.

**Sound and mood:** Heavy rain easing to drips, heron calls, chicks peeping; patient and fresh.

**Ages:** 3+

**Seeds games:** [Forest Keeper](../concepts/pages/nc-14.md), [Bird Watch](../concepts/pages/nc-04.md), [Migration Map](../concepts/pages/nc-12.md)

### 9. The wall without the Guard

**Logline:** A young adult switches off her Guard to climb a glacier wall, and an elder chooses to watch instead of stopping her.

**Setting:** A glacier valley with a granite wall above blue ice. A cold, bright morning in early spring. Physics note: each citizen may switch off their own Guard, instantly and privately, and nobody can switch it back on for them. Renewal heals injuries in days, but it cannot undo everything. Sora _(proposal)_, 19, climbs without a flight suit, so no tower can catch her.

**Beats:**

1. Dawn at the foot of the wall. Sora's Orbi says, "Guard off. Only for you." She nods.
2. She climbs alone, so that her risk stays hers. Professor Acorn sits below with a cup of tea and does not try to stop her.
3. A hold breaks. She hangs by one hand. Silence. She finds another hold.
4. At the top, the sun hits her face. She switches the Guard back on, by choice, and waves. Far below, a tiny Acorn raises his cup.
5. Evening, by a small stove. "Were you afraid?" Acorn asks. "Yes." "Good. Then it was really yours."

**Wisdom:** "Let go gently." — Letgo

**Image description:** Dramatic vertical shot of a sheer granite wall above a blue glacier on a cold, bright spring morning, painterly anime style with crisp air. Halfway up, a young woman in a tight white climbing suit with soft gray patches hangs from one hand, reaching calmly for the next hold, her face focused, chalk on her fingers. Her white sphere companion floats a few meters away, eyes dimmed to show it is only watching. Far below, on a boulder by the ice, a small old man with round spectacles in a fitted white suit holds a steaming cup. A slender white tower stands far down the valley. Palette of ice blue, granite gray, white and sunrise gold. Tense but respectful mood, no fall, no injury shown.

**Sound and mood:** Wind, breath, the scrape of fingers on rock, a single falling pebble; tense, then warm.

**Ages:** 10+

**Seeds games:** [Climbing](../concepts/pages/sm-13.md), [Choose Your Path](../concepts/pages/sa-01.md)

### 10. Why is Professor Acorn old?

**Logline:** At dusk, a child asks the only old man in the valley why his skin is wrinkled, and he tells him.

**Setting:** The words home place: a reading nest village in giant tree crowns, linked by sky-lanes. Evening, cool air, the towers glowing softly, Pim and Pom rising, small glowing insects in the leaves. Physics note: Renewal keeps adults looking 18–21 by default. Children grow up naturally to about 18. Acorn switched Renewal off long ago, so he ages.

**Beats:**

1. Children sit on cushions in a white lattice nest. Professor Acorn reads aloud.
2. Trip touches the back of his hand. "Why is your skin like a dry leaf?"
3. Acorn smiles. "I switched Renewal off, so you would know what old looks like." His Orbi shows a picture of him long ago, looking 20.
4. "Does it hurt?" "Sometimes my knees. And sometimes it is beautiful. I can feel time pass."
5. Trip looks from the picture to the old face and back. "It's the same you." Acorn laughs slowly. The towers glow.

**Wisdom:** "Two can be one, and one can be two." — Akoren

**Image description:** Cozy medium shot inside a round open nest woven from white lattice, high in a giant tree crown at dusk, hand-painted anime style. An old man with round infinity-shaped spectacles, white hair and a fitted white suit with a leaf pattern sits on a cushion, holding out his wrinkled hand. A boy of seven in a white suit with green accents touches it gently, curious. Three other children listen. A white sphere companion floats nearby and projects a soft, ghostly image of the same man, young. Through the open weave: other nests in neighboring trees, thin glowing sky-lanes, two small moons and a slender white tower with a softly glowing crown. Tiny glowing insects drift. Palette of dusk blue, warm cream, white and firefly gold. Tender, curious mood.

**Sound and mood:** Leaves, night insects, an old man's slow laugh; tender and calm.

**Ages:** 6+

**Seeds games:** [Story Dice](../concepts/pages/wl-15.md), [Cadets of Depth Academy](../concepts/pages/sa-10.md)

## Shacados scenes

### 11. Ration line at dawn

**Logline:** In a coal town before sunrise, a long line waits for gray bread, and one woman breaks hers in two.

**Setting:** A coal town in the warm belt, outside the company store. Dawn in the cold months. The sky is brown-gray smoke, turning rust-red where the dull orange sun comes up. Frost on the rails, gas flares on the horizon. The store window is barred, and a lamp burns inside. People pay with ration cards valid only here.

**Beats:**

1. A long line: women, children and old men, scarves over their faces. A Warden stands by the door, shivering like everyone else.
2. The shutter opens. A clerk stamps cards and hands out gray loaves.
3. The shutter closes before a boy at the end of the line reaches it.
4. A woman ahead of him breaks her loaf and gives him half. The Warden looks at the sky and pretends not to see.
5. The sun comes up red through the smoke. The woman and the boy walk away together, eating.

**Wisdom:** "Share what you have." — the Gentle Defaults, and every ration line

**Image description:** Wide, eye-level shot of a long line of people outside a soot-stained brick company store at dawn, gritty painterly style. A smoky brown-gray sky turns rust red near a dull orange sun. Frost covers rusted rails in the foreground. People wear layered, patched work clothes in rust, soot and mustard colors, with scarves over their mouths and worn boots; their faces are tired but dignified. In the center, a woman in a mustard scarf hands half a gray loaf to a thin boy in a too-big coat. A Warden in a shabby uniform coat stands by the door, looking away at the sky. One warm lamp-orange light glows behind the barred window. A tiny green weed grows from a crack in the step. No weapons, no violence. Palette of rust, soot, mustard, ash and lamp orange. Bleak but tender mood.

**Sound and mood:** Wind, a far factory whistle, a stamp hitting paper, bread tearing; bleak but tender.

**Ages:** 10+

**Seeds games:** [Lunch for All](../concepts/pages/ss-17.md), [Community Kitchen](../concepts/pages/nc-10.md)

### 12. The scrap dunes

**Logline:** A small girl flies a kite made of a grain sack over hills of dead machines, and Teyo helps.

**Setting:** The scrap dunes behind a rail yard: hills of rusted car bodies, boilers, wheels and wire. A windy afternoon after a dust storm. The sky is ochre with one pale band of clearer air. A single dying tree stands on a dune. Physics note: the line is thin copper wire pulled from a dead motor.

**Beats:**

1. Rakers sort metal into piles. Teyo, 15, pulls copper wire from a dead motor.
2. A raker girl, about 8, drags a kite made of a grain sack, two sticks and string. It falls again and again.
3. Teyo ties on a tail of rags and gives her his coil of wire as a line. They run down the dune.
4. The kite rises above the dust into the pale band of sky. For a moment it is the highest thing in the world.
5. The wind drops, and they reel the kite in, laughing. Teyo looks at the long wire and says, "This would make a good antenna."

**Wisdom:** "Oops means you're learning." — Oopsy

**Image description:** Wide, low-angle shot from the top of a hill of rusted scrap at windy afternoon, gritty painterly style. Dunes of corroded car bodies, broken boilers, wheels and tangled wire stretch to a horizon of smokestacks. A small girl in a patched mustard coat and goggles pushed up on her forehead runs down the slope, holding a line of shining copper wire. High above, a kite made of a patched grain sack with a tail of colorful rags flies into a pale, clearer band of sky. A lean fifteen-year-old boy in a soot-colored jacket and scarf watches and smiles. A thin dying tree stands on the dune with one small green leaf. Dust swirls in the ochre air. Palette of rust, ochre, soot, ash and one bright rag of lamp orange. Hopeful, windswept mood.

**Sound and mood:** Wind whistling through scrap, rattling metal, a child's shout of joy; windswept and hopeful.

**Ages:** 10+

**Seeds games:** [Kite Day](../concepts/pages/sm-08.md), [Recycling Sorter](../concepts/pages/nc-03.md)

### 13. The ice town

**Logline:** In a town buried in snow, a family sleeps in one room around one stove, and a neighbor brings a bucket of coal.

### 19. Fifty seconds — the station's side

**Logline:** A Rayan frontier station sees something coming and has fifty seconds to stop it without harming it.

**Setting:** A Rayan frontier asteroid station in the outer belt of Raya's system. Black space; Raya's star small and bright; white ceramic rings around a dark asteroid; an electromagnetic launch rail several kilometers long. Physics note (the [Shacados variant](first-contact.md#physics-check-of-the-pilot)): the object flies at about 10,000 km/s and is seen at 500,000 km, a 50-second window. Its light is 1.67 s old, so sensors must predict, not just look. A Rayan probe at 0.5 _c_ _(proposal)_ intercepts it about 7.8 s after launch. There is no sound in space; every sound comes from inside the station.

**Beats:**

1. Silence. The station keeper waters plants in the station garden. Her Orbi blinks: one line of light, from the dark to the station.
2. The solution-search system has already chosen, in 0.01 s: capture the object, minimize damage. The keeper has time only to read it and agree.
3. A free probe is refitted in seconds: sensors, manipulators, extra tanks. The rail glows, and the probe is gone in a white streak.
4. The probe meets a cylinder with a rounded nose. Echo scans show circuits. A correlation center finds the one controlling wire. A laser pulse is too weak; a stronger one burns the wire. The object goes into standby.
5. Hours later, the probe brings it home at 250 km/s. The keeper touches its scorched, riveted hull and sees weld marks. "Someone built this with their hands." She sends probes to find its source and make contact.

## Open questions

1. **Long trips.** Sky-lanes reach only about 2 km from a tower and towers stand 20–40 km apart, so the class in [A day on Raya](raya.md#a-day-on-raya) and in scene 3 rides around one tower. Should a few favorite routes get towers close enough to chain their lanes, or do long trips stay on foot, by boat or by glider?
2. **Pim and Pom.** Are the moons small enough that a double eclipse is always partial (scene 4)? Which one looks bigger, and how often do they cross the star together?
3. **The tower song as a round.** Is a song written around the 90-second delay of sound between towers (scene 7) a good festival rule?
4. **Tower assemblers.** Are swarms of fist-sized white assemblers the right picture of how a tower grows (scene 6)?
5. **Probe control.** We chose an uncrewed probe that steers itself with a relay autopilot and is only tracked from the ground (scenes 14 and 20); no crew follows it. Is a pilot firing the climb to orbit by hand (scene 14) a believable reason for Kesh's refusal?
6. **Rayan contact probes.** Should Rayan probes show the Orbi's red-left, blue-right lights as a visual link, or stay plain white?
7. **Light delay to Shacados.** We chose a wide binary 2,000 AU apart ([start systems](first-contact.md#the-two-civilizations)): 11.6 days each way between the worlds, about a night to the Rayan contact probe parked near Shacados (scenes 16, 20 and 21). Is that the right distance?
8. **New names.** Sora (scene 9) is a proposal. The station keeper, the tracking crew and the raker girl stay unnamed until the maintainer decides.
9. **Age ratings.** Are Shacados scenes with children in hard places (scenes 11, 12, 13, 17) right at 10+? Should the station's side of the pilot (scene 19), which shows no harm, be 10+ instead of 13+?
