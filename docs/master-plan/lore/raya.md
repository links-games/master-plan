# Raya — the home world

Raya is the planet of the peaceful, advanced civilization of
[First Contact](first-contact.md) and the home of everyone who plays our
games. This document turns the maintainer's
[Raya comment](https://github.com/links-games/master-plan/pull/2#issuecomment-6338585632)
(requirements R55–R65 in the
[case study](../../case-studies/issue-1/requirements.md#raya-shacados-toddlers-and-the-website-r55r76))
into a detailed, checkable setting. The real science behind each technology,
and the honest list of places where the lore goes beyond it, is in the
[Raya and Shacados research note](../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md).

> The landscape of home planet Raya should be untouched nature and a lot of
> tall white acoustic transmission and telecom towers. Characters should
> wear futuristic clothes that are mainly white, and tight. Characters should
> walk, play, fly acoustically with or without spherical white robotic
> companions […] Spheres give characters an ability of telekinesis […]
> Society is highly ethical and moral by default and in harmony with nature.
> Laws are entirely optional, but defaults are peaceful. — the maintainer

Spelling: the earlier comment wrote **Reya**. We use **Raya** (people:
**Rayans**), the spelling of the latest comment. Every document in the
master plan now uses it, except for verbatim quotes.

Status: **draft for maintainer review.** Every name, number or mechanism
that the maintainer did not state is marked _(proposal)_ or listed in
[open questions](#open-questions).

## The planet

- **Star and orbit** _(proposal)_: Raya orbits a quiet yellow star a little
  older than the Sun. Its year is 401 Rayan days and its day is 26 hours.
  Two small moons, **Pim** and **Pom**, give gentle tides and double
  eclipses, a favorite sight in the [scenery pilots](scenery-pilots.md).
  The brightest point in the night sky, as bright as a half moon, is
  Shacados' orange star, 11.6 light-days away in a wide binary with Raya's
  star ([start systems](first-contact.md#the-two-civilizations)).
- **Climate:** slightly warmer and wetter than Earth's, with a long green
  spring. Rayans never industrialized the way Earth did, or they cleaned it
  up so long ago that no trace remains. The air is clear enough to see
  mountains 200 km away, and the night sky is dark: tower lights are dimmed
  and shielded by default for the animals.
- **Biomes:** temperate rainforests of blue-green giants; flower steppes
  where herds migrate; mangrove deltas; glacier valleys; coral shallows; red
  rock canyons; quiet islands. All of it is "untouched": no fences, no
  fields, no roads, no dams.
- **Wildlife:** rich and unafraid of people, because no Rayan has hunted for
  thousands of years. Children grow up with deer-like grazers at the edge of
  the garden and seabirds that land on an outstretched arm.
- **Population** _(proposal)_: about 90 million people. The number is stable
  because nobody dies of age and births are rare and planned.

### How people live in untouched nature

Rayans do not build cities, roads or farms. Their footprint is designed to
be almost invisible:

1. **Nests** _(proposal)_ are light homes woven from white lattice and grown
   wood. They sit in tree crowns, on cliff ledges or under the rings of a
   tower, and they are built and moved by levitation, without heavy
   machines. A nest touches the ground in a few points or not at all.
2. **Food** comes from gardens of wild-looking perennial plants, from
   foraging what animals do not need, and from **copies** (see the
   [Orbis](#orbis--the-companion-spheres)). No animal is killed for food.
   Rayans have the technology to copy meat, but most simply prefer plants.
3. **Water** is taken from rain, not from rivers; rivers belong to the
   rivers.
4. **Energy** comes from solar skins on the towers and deep geothermal
   wells under them. It travels as light and sound between towers, so there
   are no power lines.
5. **Waste** does not exist as a category. Everything is disassembled by
   assemblers into air, water and minerals.

## The Chord towers

Tall white towers are the only large human-made structures on Raya. Their
network is the **Chord**, the "telepathy internet" of the maintainer's
comment.

| Property     | Value _(proposal)_                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Height       | 300–1,200 m. The tallest stand on plains, the shortest on ridges.                                                                                                            |
| Spacing      | Every 20–40 km, so each tower can see the next over the horizon. A tower 600 m tall sees a horizon about 87 km away (horizon distance ≈ 3.57 × √height in m, in km).         |
| Look         | A slender white ceramic spire, slightly twisted like a plant stem. Rings of acoustic emitters stand out like the nodes of bamboo. A soft light shines at the crown at night. |
| Footprint    | Three root-like feet on bedrock, about 30 m across in total. The ground around them stays wild.                                                                              |
| Functions    | Telecom (light and radio links between towers); acoustic **sky-lanes** for Chord flight; energy collection and storage; weather and ecosystem sensing; Orbi charging.        |
| Sound        | The emitters work in ultrasound, above human hearing. Audible "tower songs" are played only at festivals, and never during animal breeding seasons.                          |
| Construction | Grown layer by layer by assemblers from local stone and air, with no roads or cranes. A tower takes about a year to grow.                                                    |

**Sky-lanes.** Around each tower, the arrays shape invisible channels in
the air. Towers stand 20–40 km apart, so the lanes of neighboring towers do
not meet: long trips are walked, or made by boat or glider. A person in a flight suit can glide along them at
walking to cycling speed, 5–40 km/h. Flight works only within about 2 km of
a tower, and it is never fast: the lore keeps it gentle and believable
(lore exception E1 in the
[research note](../../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-raya-shacados.md#1-acoustic-levitation-and-acoustic-flying)).
Rayans call it "flying acoustically", or just "riding the Chord".

## Rayans

### Who they are

Rayans are humanlike. Because of [Renewal](#renewal--forever-young), almost
every adult looks 18–21 years old. Real ages range from 18 to many
thousands of years. They are calm, curious and very polite. They speak
aloud with friends, even though the Chord would let them think to each
other, because they love voices.

### Clothing — the white lattice

| Rule     | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Color    | Mainly white (canon). Small accents in the [role colors](README.md#visual-rules): coral red at the left wrist, sky blue at the right wrist, green on the collar or belt.                |
| Fit      | Tight and futuristic (canon), like a sleek sports suit. Modest and non-sexualized in every age tier: long sleeves or soft panels, no cutouts, no emphasis on body shape.                |
| Material | **Lattice** _(proposal)_: a self-cleaning, temperature-adjusting smart fabric. Its rigid micro-panels catch the tower fields for flight, and it closes into a rain shell.               |
| Variants | Daily suit; flight suit with visible pearl panels on the back, arms and shins; festival suit whose lattice glows with slow white light patterns; field suit with soft gray mud patches. |
| Children | Same suits in looser cuts, with bigger accent shapes for recognition.                                                                                                                   |
| Elders   | The same fitted white suits, often with a soft pattern (leaves, waves) woven into the lattice. Nobody tells them what to wear.                                                          |

Why white? _(proposal)_ White lattice reflects the towers' fields evenly,
shows the wearer's mood through faint color glows when they want it to, and
looks clean against green and blue nature. It is also a cultural sign:
"I bring nothing that harms".

### Orbis — the companion spheres

Orbis are the spherical white robotic companions of the comment. Most
Rayans have one; some share one; some prefer none.

| Property    | Value _(proposal unless marked canon)_                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Look        | A smooth white sphere about 30 cm across, floating at shoulder height. A soft ring of light marks its "equator". Two glowing eyes appear on its surface when it talks: coral red on the left and sky blue on the right. |
| Role        | Electromagnetic transmitter for the telepathy internet (canon). It reads and writes thought-level messages with consent, and relays them to the Chord.                                                                  |
| Telekinesis | Lifts, holds and turns objects up to a few kilograms within about a meter, using diamagnetic and acoustic fields (canon idea; limits are our proposal, lore exception E2).                                              |
| Scanning    | Sees inside objects with sound and fields, without touching them. Used for copying, medicine and science.                                                                                                               |
| Guard       | By default, it can pause a person's muscles to prevent harm (canon). See [the Guard](#the-guard--thoughts-are-free).                                                                                                    |
| Renewal     | Runs the wellness treatment that keeps bodies young (canon idea). See [Renewal](#renewal--forever-young).                                                                                                               |
| Personality | Quiet and helpful, with a gentle sense of humor. An Orbi is not a person and says so. It never pretends to have feelings it does not have.                                                                              |
| Power       | Recharges near any tower; a full charge lasts a day of normal use.                                                                                                                                                      |

**The link to the Links.** The red-left, blue-right eyes are the face of
[Linky](characters.md#1-linky--the-hero) and the linksplatform avatar. In
the kids' layer, a child's Orbi shows its face as a Link. The Link creatures
of [Omi](README.md#the-world-omi) are how Rayan children see the Chord:
every thread of Omi is a real connection in the network
([details](#how-raya-connects-to-omi)).

**The apple, the canonical example.** A child wants an apple from a wild
tree. Her Orbi floats up and scans the apple through its skin. It holds it
still in a diamagnetic field so it does not swing, and it records every
cell. The apple never leaves its branch. That night, the garden assembler at
home builds an identical apple from air, water and compost, using tower
energy. In the morning the child eats her copy, and the original stays for
the birds. Rayans call it the **first courtesy**: _copy, don't take_
(lore exception E3; one night is the honest price in energy and time).

### The Chord — the telepathy internet

- **What it is.** A planet-wide network of Orbis and towers that carries
  speech, images, feelings and intentions between minds, with consent.
- **How it feels.** Like hearing a friend's voice inside your head, or
  seeing a picture they imagine. It is never like reading a mind without
  permission.
- **Consent.** Every connection is opened by both sides. A Rayan can be
  "quiet" (closed to everyone), "open to friends", or "open to all", and
  can change this with a thought.
- **Privacy.** The Chord carries only what the sender chooses to send.
  Orbis are built so that they cannot read thoughts the person did not
  address to someone. This is the base of the society's trust.
- **Quiet people.** Some Rayans live without an Orbi, or keep theirs on
  "quiet" for years. This is respected. They can still talk, write and
  visit, the old ways.
- **Limits.** The Chord moves at the speed of light between towers, so a
  message across the planet arrives in a fraction of a second. It reaches
  space stations with a real light delay of seconds to hours, which matters
  in [First Contact](first-contact.md#physics-check-of-the-pilot).

### The Guard — thoughts are free

By default, an Orbi watches the movements of its person through the Chord.
If a movement would injure someone — a child about to fall from a cliff,
an angry push, a careless step onto a nest — the Orbi **pauses the motor
command** for a moment. The person feels a soft "hold". The movement
does not happen.

- **Thoughts are never touched.** The Guard pauses muscles. It does not
  change what anyone thinks, feels or wants. A Rayan may be furious, may
  imagine anything, and may say "no". This is the maintainer's "thoughts
  remain free", and Rayans treat it as sacred.
- **It protects from self-harm and harm to others** by default.
- **Each citizen can switch it off for themselves** (canon). They do it for
  sports, adventure, art, or simply out of principle. Switching it off is
  instant and private.
- **It cannot be switched off for someone else.** Your choice changes your
  own Guard; every other citizen still controls theirs. A personal waiver
  does not speak for anyone else (see [waivers](#waivers--laws-are-optional)).
- **It does not punish.** There are no prisons on Raya. A paused movement is
  the whole response. If someone keeps trying to harm, friends come to talk,
  listen and help.
- **Children.** The same personal choice belongs to every citizen. The Guard
  starts on, and nobody, including a parent, can waive it for another person.
  Games for young children present gentle, cooperative play and keep physical
  risk demonstrations in their older age tiers. This product choice does not
  change the fictional citizen’s rights.

### Renewal — forever young

- **Canon:** citizens are immortal and stay forever young, looking 18–21.
  Old people are rare: only those who disabled the automatic wellness
  treatment.
- **How it works** _(proposal, lore exception E5)_: Orbis run a slow,
  continuous repair of every cell. Injuries heal in days, and illnesses are
  caught before they start.
- **Children** grow up naturally from birth to about 18, and Renewal starts
  only then. Childhood is never paused, because Rayans think every person
  deserves a whole childhood.
- **Elders.** Some Rayans switch Renewal off to feel time, to keep a promise,
  or to grow old with a partner who did. Elders are rare and deeply
  respected. [Professor Acorn](characters.md#anime-hosts-r30) is one of
  them: he chose to age so that children would know what "old" looks like.
- **Death** is rare but real. Accidents can happen to people who switched
  off the Guard, and some elders choose a natural end. Rayan stories treat
  death gently and honestly, but **never in the toddler or children
  tiers.**

## Society

### The Gentle Defaults

Rayans call their only code the **Gentle Defaults** _(proposal name)_. It
is short enough for a child to remember:

1. **Do no harm** — to others, to yourself, to nature.
2. **Ask first** — every touch, every connection, every change needs a yes.
3. **Copy, don't take** — leave the world as whole as you found it.
4. **Share what you have** — energy, knowledge, time.
5. **Thoughts are free** — nobody reads, changes or judges a thought
   that was not shared.

Everyone starts with these defaults, and everyone is free to change how they
apply to themselves. That is what "laws are optional" means: no one can force
a rule on you, and you cannot force your own choices on anyone else.

### Waivers — laws are optional

| Kind                | Who                             | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Personal waiver** | One citizen, for themselves     | Switches off a default for oneself. Examples: no Guard when climbing free solo; no Renewal; being "quiet" on the Chord.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Mutual waiver**   | Two citizens (canon), instantly | Both agree through the Chord to waive any rule between them. Examples: a full-contact wrestling match; a game of pranks; a duel of insults for fun. Either one can end it with a thought.                                                                                     |
| **Group waiver**    | A circle of citizens            | The same, for a team game or an expedition. Anyone can leave at any time, and the waiver ends for them.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Consent scope**   | Everyone affected               | A waiver changes the participants' own defaults. It does not give consent on behalf of outsiders, wild animals or shared nature. A citizen can still disable their own Guard entirely; this is a real freedom, with real consequences, not an absolute technical ban on harm. |

Real-world parallels: informed consent in medicine, and the voluntary
rules of contact sports, where boxers agree to blows that would be assault
in the street. Rayans just make consent instant, mutual and revocable.

### Why it works (and where it could fail)

A realistic society needs friction. These are the tensions Rayan stories
explore:

- **Without punishment, what stops a bad actor?** The Guard stops the act by
  default, the Chord makes help quick, and the culture makes harm unthinkable for
  most people. For the rare few, the answer is patience, and that is not
  always enough. Stories may show it.
- **Boredom of forever.** Some immortals drift. Rayans answer with art,
  science and care for nature, and with long journeys: some cross the sea
  by sail, without flight, for a year.
- **Too safe to grow?** Young Rayans switch off the Guard to feel real risk.
  Elders worry. Both are respected.
- **The quiet minority.** People who leave the Chord can feel left out,
  even when nobody excludes them.
- **Strength without violence.** Raya is powerful enough to destroy
  Shacados and will never do it. The [First Contact](first-contact.md)
  story asks whether gentleness can survive meeting cruelty.

### Economy, work and learning

- **Resources, not money.** Energy, matter and attention are shared. A
  Rayan asks the Chord for what they need, and the community balances
  needs with nature's limits. See [economy](../economy.md).
- **Work** is chosen. People garden, care for animals, study, teach,
  compose, explore space, run the frontier stations and mend the
  ecosystem. "What do you care for?" replaces "What do you do?".
- **Learning.** Children are rare, so each one is surrounded by many
  teachers. They learn through play, and much of that play happens in
  **Omi**, the gentle learning world of the Chord, which is what our games
  show.
- **Science** is a joyful public practice: anyone can open an experiment
  on the Chord and invite others to watch.

## A day on Raya

> Dawn. Mira, 9, wakes in a nest of white lattice high in a blue-green
> tree. Her Orbi, Pip, drifts over with two sleepy eyes, one red and one
> blue. They climb down the nest ladder to the meadow, where a herd of grazers moves
> through the mist. Pip shows her the herd's calves as tiny glowing links in
> her mind; she counts them and gets it right this time.
>
> At the edge of the forest, Mira wants an apple from the old tree. "Ask
> first," says Pip. She asks the tree, which means she checks that no bird
> is nesting there. Pip scans the apple without picking it. "Tomorrow," Pip
> says. "Tonight the garden will make one."
>
> In the afternoon her teacher, who looks 20 and is 1,400 years old, takes
> the class, in flight suits, riding the Chord around the valley tower. They glide at the speed of
> a bicycle over a river full of salmon-like fish. Theo tries to push Mira
> into the water as a joke, and his arm simply stops — the Guard. He
> laughs, embarrassed. Mira grins and says, through the Chord, "Next time
> ask, and I'll push you back."
>
> In the evening the towers glow softly. Professor Acorn, the only old man
> in the valley, tells the children about a dark, rusty planet called
> Shacados, but only the hopeful part, about the people there who share
> their bread.

## How Raya connects to Omi

- **Omi is a Rayan learning world inside the Chord.** When a Rayan child
  plays, their Orbi shows the network as Omi: a round, soft planet woven
  from glowing threads, where every being is a Link. The rules of Omi are
  the rules of links, and so the rules of the Chord.
- **Linky is the face of an Orbi.** A child's Orbi wears Linky's red and
  blue eyes. When the child grows up, the Orbi stays, and the face becomes
  simpler.
- **The hosts are young Rayans.** [Lina, Doubl and Trip, and Ana](characters.md#anime-hosts-r30)
  wear white lattice suits with their accent colors, and each has an Orbi.
  Professor Acorn is an elder who chose to age.
- **Toddlers see only Omi and Raya's meadows.** They never see Shacados.

## Open questions

1. **Names.** Orbi, the Chord, lattice, nests, the Guard, Renewal and the
   Gentle Defaults are working names. Pim and Pom, the moons, too.
2. **Population and births.** Is a stable 90 million with rare births the
   right picture?
3. **Food.** Do Rayans eat copied meat, or only plants?
4. **Flight limits.** Is Chord flight near towers only, at bicycle speed,
   the right balance between wonder and realism?
5. **Death.** May stories for teens and adults show the death of a Rayan
   who switched off the Guard?
