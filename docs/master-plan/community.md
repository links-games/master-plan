# Community — Global, Kind, Open

Covers requirements **R26, R27, R28** and **D4**.
Evidence (donation platform fees, merch services, open-source game
communities) is in
[research-market-games.md](../case-studies/issue-1/data/research-market-games.md)
sections 5.3–5.6.

## Who the community is

| Group               | What they want                           | What we give them                                                        |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Players (13+)       | Fun, daily puzzles, collections, sharing | Daily challenge with spoiler-free share cards, events, sticker packs     |
| Parents             | Safe, useful games for their children    | Parent dashboard, printable certificates, transparent privacy            |
| Teachers            | Lessons that fit a classroom             | Classroom mode, lesson plans per game, no accounts needed                |
| Contributors        | Meaningful open work, credit, learning   | Good-first issues, AI-assisted pull requests, credits in games, bounties |
| Artists and writers | A cute universe to create in             | Contests, the lore repository, fan-art guidelines, credits               |
| Translators         | Bring games to their language            | `i18n` repository, glossary of the lore, credits                         |
| Supporters          | Help the games exist                     | Transparent budget, supporter badge, early access                        |

Children are part of the audience, not of the community channels: no open
chat for children anywhere, emoji-only reactions inside games, and family
leaderboards only.

## Growth loops

- **Daily puzzle and share card:** one shared seed per day, a share card
  that shows the result without spoilers (our own format, not Wordle's).
- **Characters as content:** stickers, GIFs and short animations of the
  Links travel without the game; every sticker links to the hub.
- **Fan art and contests:** "draw a Link" contests with winners added to
  the game as cosmetics (with a CC0 contributor agreement).
- **Level and puzzle contests** for games with editors (Link It!, Pixel
  Itsy).
- **Classroom mode:** teachers recommend us to other teachers and parents.
- **Localization by the community:** each new language opens a new market
  and improves the learning content.
- **Contributor credits in-game:** every merged pull request and every
  translation is credited in the "About" screen.

## Contributors

- Every repository has `CONTRIBUTING.md`, issue and pull request templates
  from `links-games/.github`, and labels `good first issue`,
  `help wanted`, `ai-solve`, `not-adorable`.
- **AI-assisted contributions:** anyone can open an issue; maintainers add
  `ai-solve` and the [AI pipeline](ai-pipeline.md) drafts a pull request.
  Human contributors review, test on devices and polish — the work that
  makes games adorable.
- **Code of conduct** with an emphasis on kindness and child safety.
- **Recognition:** monthly "Link of the month" for a contributor, credits
  in games, contributor stickers.

## Bounties

From Phase 3, or earlier if donations allow:

- Bounties on issues through Algora (the funder pays a 9–11% fee; the
  contributor gets 100% of the bounty) or Open Collective expenses.
- Typical bounties: a new platform adapter, a new language, a level pack,
  a character animation, accessibility fixes.
- Bounties are public, with fixed amounts and acceptance criteria in the
  issue.

## Donations

Open from Phase 1 on the website and the "About" screen of each game
(behind a parental gate in kids mode).

| Platform        | Fee (see research)                   | Use                                              |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| GitHub Sponsors | 0% from personal accounts            | Primary, for developers and companies            |
| Open Collective | Free platform; fiscal host fees vary | Transparent public budget; expenses for bounties |
| Ko-fi           | 0% on one-off donations              | Low-friction tips from players                   |
| Boosty          | Check current fee                    | Russian-speaking community                       |
| itch.io         | Adjustable revenue share             | Pay-what-you-want for desktop builds             |

Donors receive a thank-you sticker pack and, if they want, a name in the
credits. Donations never unlock gameplay.

## Merchandise

Merch follows the audience — it starts only after a character is loved
(measured by sticker shares and fan art).

1. **Free first (Phase 2):** printable coloring pages, wallpapers,
   Telegram and messenger sticker packs. Good for reach and for classrooms.
2. **Print-on-demand (Phase 3):** stickers, pins, T-shirts, mugs and
   notebooks through Printful or Spring — no inventory, no upfront cost.
3. **Plush and toys (Phase 3+):** only through licensed manufacturers with
   toy safety certification (EN 71 / CE in the EU, CPSIA in the US, UKCA in
   the UK). The Links are designed from simple primitives, so they make
   good plush.
4. **Licensing (Phase 4):** licensees for books, toys and apparel once the
   characters have an audience.

Merch uses the trademarked names and designs, so only official channels
and licensees can sell it ([legal](legal.md#names-and-trademarks)).

## Hiring

When the budget allows (R27) — revenue or funding covering at least 12
months of the salary — in this order:

| #   | Role                                    | Why first                                                             |
| --- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Character artist / animator (2D + Rive) | Adorability is the product; the cast needs one consistent hand.       |
| 2   | Game designer with education background | Learning objectives, difficulty curves, pre/post checks.              |
| 3   | Community manager (multilingual)        | Channels, contests, moderation, parent and teacher relations.         |
| 4   | Mobile release engineer                 | Store compliance (kids categories), builds for every shell, payments. |
| 5   | 3D artist                               | Phase 4: toy-like 3D cast and anime-shaded hosts.                     |
| 6   | Writer / lore editor                    | Episodes, story games, localization of lore.                          |

Principles:

- **Hire from the community first** — contributors with merged work have
  already passed the best interview.
- **Remote and global**, paid fairly by role, not by location where
  possible.
- **Paid trial tasks** taken from real issues.
- **Contractors before employees** until revenue is predictable.

## Moderation and safety

- No open chat for children; moderated channels for adults.
- Fan content involving the characters must follow the
  [tone guide](lore/README.md#tone-of-voice).
- Reports go to a public safety contact on the website; child-safety
  reports are handled first.
